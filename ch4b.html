Chapter 4(B): Avoiding Moral Vices

Prejudice, Self-preference, Foul Language, Intoxicants, Bribery & Corruption

🎯 Learning Outcomes

After studying this lesson, students will be able to:

  • Understand the meaning and concept of moral vices — prejudice, self-preference, foul language, use of intoxicants, bribery and corruption.
  • Know the condemnation of these moral vices in light of the Quran and Sunnah.
  • Learn about the avoidance of these vices from the Seerat-e-Nabawi ﷺ, the Seerat of Ahl-e-Bait and the Sahaba (RA).
  • Review the social harms these vices cause and how they can be remedied.
  • Aware of the Quranic and Prophetic condemnation, avoid these vices in daily life.
  • Play a role in improving society by avoiding moral vices in everyday dealings.
• • •

What Are Moral Vices?

Moral Vices (Akhlaqi Razaa'il): "Razaa'il" is an Arabic word derived from "Razeelah," meaning bad, mean, or despicable. Moral vices are evil habits and behaviours that cause social corruption, disorder and punishment in the Hereafter.

Below are the major moral vices that Islam condemns and commands Muslims to avoid.

• • •

1. Prejudice (Taa'ssub)

Prejudice (Taa'ssub): Unjustly supporting one's own group, party, tribe or relatives without distinguishing between right and wrong, truth and falsehood.

Islam, in contrast to prejudice, teaches Islamic brotherhood and equality. To unite Muslims into a single community and thread them on the string of unity, prejudice has been demolished and equality has been commanded.

يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ إِنَّا خَلَقْنَاكُم مِّن ذَكَرٍ وَأُنثَىٰ وَجَعَلْنَاكُمْ شُعُوبًا وَقَبَائِلَ لِتَعَارَفُوا
"O mankind! Indeed, We have created you from a male and a female, and made you into nations and tribes so that you may know one another."
— Surah Al-Hujurat 49:13

🕋 Khutbah Hajjat-ul-Wida (Farewell Sermon)

The Prophet ﷺ, while teaching equality and brotherhood, declared:

"You are all children of Adam, and Adam was created from clay. None of you has any superiority over another except through Taqwa (God-consciousness)."

Because of Islam's teachings of equality, the Sahaba (RA) demonstrated unparalleled brotherhood at the occasion of Muwakhat-e-Madinah (the Brotherhood of Madinah).

"وہ شخص ہم میں سے نہیں جو کسی عصیبت کی طرف بلائے، وہ شخص بھی ہم میں سے نہیں جو عصیبت کی بنیاد پر لڑائی لڑے، اور وہ شخص بھی ہم میں سے نہیں جو تعصب کا تصور لیے ہوئے مرے۔"
"He is not one of us who calls towards tribalism; he is not one of us who fights on the basis of tribalism; and he is not one of us who dies holding onto tribalism."
— Sunan Abu Dawood: 5121

⚠️ Social Harms of Prejudice

  • The distinction between right and wrong is destroyed.
  • Unity, agreement and brotherhood come to an end.
  • Killing, violence and corruption become widespread.
  • The fabric of national unity is torn apart.
  • Evils like selfishness and self-interest are born.

As Pakistanis, we must rise above provincial, linguistic and religious prejudices and play our role in the progress and prosperity of Pakistan.

• • •

2. Self-Preference (Khud Pasandi)

Self-Preference (Khud Pasandi): A deadly disease that corrupts a person's character. The self-preferred person only likes themselves, gives preference to their own thinking and personality over others, considers themselves the best and most superior, and desires to be praised, honoured and respected everywhere.

Self-preference leads a person into self-interest, hypocrisy (Riya) and arrogance (Takabbur).

وَلَا تَحْسَبَنَّ الَّذِيْنَ يَفْرَحُوْنَ بِمَا اَتَوْا وَّيُحِبُّوْنَ اَنْ يُّحْمَدُوْا بِمَا لَمْ يَفْعَلُوْا فَلَا تَحْسَبَنَّهُمْ بِمَفَازَةٍ مِّنَ الْعَذَابِ
"And do not think that those who rejoice in what they have done and love to be praised for what they did not do — do not think them safe from punishment."
— Surah Aal-e-Imran 3:188

🌿 The Example of the Sahaba & Ahl-e-Bait

The Sahaba (RA) and the noble Ahl-e-Bait (RA), trained by the Prophet ﷺ, disliked self-preference. They always adopted humility and modesty, attributed every blessing to Allah, and remained perpetually grateful to Allah.

• • •

3. Foul Language (Fahsh Goi)

Foul Language (Fahsh Goi): Vulgar speech, swearing, and immoral conversation. It is among the sins of the tongue.

Bad language does not only mean swearing at someone — it includes every word or sentence that causes hurt, distress or pain to anyone in any way.

"کامل مسلمان وہ ہے کہ جس کی زبان اور ہاتھ سے دوسرے مسلمان محفوظ ہوں۔"
"A complete Muslim is one from whose tongue and hand other Muslims are safe."
— Sahih Bukhari: 10

Islam forbids calling others by bad names or offensive titles. It also discourages foul language and useless arguments and debates, commanding Muslims to avoid them.

وَلَا تَقْرَبُوا الْفَوَاحِشَ مَا ظَهَرَ مِنْهَا وَمَا بَطَنَ
"And do not approach immoralities — what is apparent of them and what is concealed."
— Surah Al-An'am 6:151
• • •

4. Intoxicants (Nashiyat)

Intoxicants (Nashiyat): All substances that cause intoxication. Intoxication paralyses the nerves and gives rise to numerous moral vices.

Allah has granted humans the ability to think, reason and distinguish between good and evil. Intoxication destroys this ability — a person can no longer tell right from wrong. This is why every intoxicating substance is declared Haram in Islam.

يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِيْنَ آمَنُوْا إِنَّمَا الْخَمْرُ وَالْمَيْسِرُ وَالْأَنْصَابُ وَالْأَزْلَامُ رِجْسٌ مِّنْ عَمَلِ الشَّيْطَانِ فَاجْتَنِبُوْهُ لَعَلَّكُمْ تُفْلِحُوْنَ
"O you who believe! Intoxicants, gambling, idolatry and divining arrows are abominations of Satan's handiwork, so avoid them that you may prosper."
— Surah Al-Ma'idah 5:90
"ہر نشہ آور چیز شراب ہے اور ہر نشہ حرام ہے۔"
"Every intoxicating thing is Khamr (wine), and every intoxicant is Haram."
— Sahih Muslim: 2003

⚠️ Modern Dangers

  • Intoxicants cause countless long-term diseases and moral evils — leading to both individual and collective corruption.
  • Cigarettes serve as the first step towards harder drugs like charas (hashish) and opium.
  • Smoking causes cancer and other fatal diseases.
  • In the present era, the use of shisha (hookah) is rapidly increasing among the youth, which is extremely harmful to their health.

We must stay away from all things that cause intoxication.

• • •

5. Bribery & Corruption (Rishwat Sitaani & Bad Unwaani)

Corruption (Bad Unwaani): Any act in which someone's right is usurped and benefit is given to a person who does not deserve it.
Bribery (Rishwat): A specific form of corruption in which a gift, present or payment is given to a person in authority to obtain wrongful gain. Usually, in government or private disputes, compensation is paid to secure unjust advantage — resulting in the deprivation of the rightful person's rights.

⚠️ Harms of Bribery & Corruption

  • Spreads despair, deviation, oppression and injustice among the people.
  • Social peace and tranquillity are destroyed.
  • At the national level, power and authority fall into the hands of unqualified people who gamble with the nation's interests for personal gain.

Forms of corruption include: betrayal (Khiyanat), dishonesty (Bad Diyanti), embezzlement (Ghabn) and manipulation (Hera Pheri).

وَلَا تَأْكُلُوْا أَمْوَالَكُم بَيْنَكُم بِالْبَاطِلِ وَتُدْلُوْا بِهَا إِلَى الْحُكَّامِ لِتَأْكُلُوْا فَرِيْقًا مِّنْ أَمْوَالِ النَّاسِ بِالْإِثْمِ وَأَنْتُمْ تَعْلَمُوْنَ
"And do not consume one another's wealth unjustly, nor offer it as bribery to the rulers so that you may knowingly consume a portion of people's wealth in sin."
— Surah Al-Baqarah 2:188
"نبی کریم ﷺ نے رشوت دینے والے اور لینے والے دونوں پر لعنت فرمائی ہے۔"
"The Prophet ﷺ cursed both the giver and the taker of bribes equally."
— Sunan Abu Dawood: 3580

🏛️ Merit in the Prophetic & Rashidun Era

Government officials often accept bribes under the guise of "gifts" — but the Prophet ﷺ also forbade accepting gifts acquired during official duty.

The Prophet ﷺ always appointed the most qualified person to any responsibility or office. The Khulafa-e-Rashideen (RA) upheld the same principle — bribery, nepotism and dishonesty were actively discouraged. This is precisely why, in an extremely short period, the borders of the State of Madinah expanded from Africa to Asia and from Asia to Europe.

• • •

Conclusion: The Islamic Remedy

Islam is the champion of world peace and uproots every habit that stands as an obstacle to it. Alongside condemning intoxicants, foul language and prejudice, Islam has issued severe warnings against bribery and corruption.

Islam lit such a true light of selflessness, sacrifice, equality and brotherhood among people that bribery is eliminated at its very root.

🌟 The Path to Progress

If we wish to progress today and reclaim our lost status, we must adopt these golden Prophetic principles and free ourselves from prejudice, self-preference, foul language, intoxicants, bribery and corruption.

Question 2: Short Answers

(i) Write one Quranic command condemning self-preference. ▼

Allah says in the Quran: "And do not think that those who rejoice in what they have done and love to be praised for what they did not do — do not think them safe from punishment" (Surah Aal-e-Imran 3:188). This verse condemns those who take pride in achievements they did not earn and crave praise for deeds they never performed — a clear description of self-preference and vanity.

(ii) Write any two modern forms of intoxicants. ▼
  1. Cigarettes and tobacco: These serve as the first step towards harder drugs like charas (hashish) and opium. Smoking causes cancer and other fatal diseases.
  2. Shisha (hookah): Its use is rapidly increasing among the youth in the present era and is extremely harmful to their health, causing respiratory and cardiovascular diseases.
(iii) Write any two social harms of prejudice. ▼
  1. Destruction of unity and brotherhood: Prejudice tears apart the fabric of national unity, ending agreement and brotherhood among people. The distinction between right and wrong is erased.
  2. Spread of violence and corruption: Prejudice leads to killing, violence and widespread disorder in society. Evils like selfishness and self-interest take root, destroying social peace.
(iv) How was merit promoted in the era of the Prophet ﷺ and the Khulafa-e-Rashideen (RA)? ▼

The Prophet ﷺ always appointed the most qualified and deserving person to any responsibility or office, regardless of their tribe, family or social status. The Khulafa-e-Rashideen (RA) strictly upheld the same principle — bribery, nepotism and dishonesty were actively discouraged. No one was given a position based on personal connections or wealth. This merit-based system is precisely why, in an extremely short period, the borders of the State of Madinah expanded from Africa to Asia and from Asia to Europe.

(v) How does bribery usurp the rights of others? ▼

Bribery is a form of corruption in which a gift, payment or favour is given to a person in authority to obtain wrongful gain. This directly usurps the rights of others because:

  • The rightful and deserving person is deprived of their due — for example, a qualified candidate loses a job to an unqualified person who paid a bribe.
  • In legal disputes, justice is denied to the innocent party because the judge or official favours the one who paid.
  • At the national level, power falls into unqualified hands, and those in authority gamble with the nation's interests for personal gain.

The Prophet ﷺ cursed both the giver and taker of bribes (Abu Dawood: 3580), and the Quran forbids consuming people's wealth through bribery (Al-Baqarah 2:188).

Question 3: Detailed Notes

Write a detailed note on Prejudice (Taa'ssub). ▼

Definition

Prejudice (Taa'ssub) means unjustly supporting one's own group, party, tribe or relatives without distinguishing between right and wrong. It is blind loyalty that ignores truth and justice.

Islamic Teaching

Islam demolished prejudice and replaced it with Islamic brotherhood and equality. The Quran declares that all humans are created from one man and one woman, and nations and tribes exist only for mutual recognition — not superiority (Al-Hujurat 49:13). The Prophet ﷺ stated in the Farewell Sermon that no one has superiority over another except through Taqwa.

Prophetic Condemnation

The Prophet ﷺ declared: "He is not one of us who calls towards tribalism, who fights on the basis of tribalism, or who dies holding tribalism" (Abu Dawood: 5121).

Social Harms

Prejudice destroys the distinction between right and wrong, ends unity and brotherhood, causes killing and corruption, scatters national unity and breeds selfishness. As Pakistanis, we must rise above provincial, linguistic and religious prejudices.

Write a detailed note on Intoxicants (Nashiyat). ▼

Definition

Intoxicants (Nashiyat) are all substances that cause intoxication. They paralyse the nerves and destroy the God-given ability to think, reason and distinguish good from evil.

Quranic Prohibition

Allah declares: "O you who believe! Intoxicants, gambling, idolatry and divining arrows are abominations of Satan's handiwork, so avoid them that you may prosper" (Al-Ma'idah 5:90).

Prophetic Prohibition

The Prophet ﷺ said: "Every intoxicating thing is Khamr (wine), and every intoxicant is Haram" (Muslim: 2003). This blanket ruling covers all forms of intoxication regardless of the substance.

Modern Dangers

Intoxicants cause countless long-term diseases and moral evils. Cigarettes serve as the first step towards harder drugs like charas and opium. Smoking causes cancer. Shisha use is rapidly increasing among youth and is extremely harmful. All intoxicating substances must be completely avoided.

Write a detailed note on Bribery & Corruption (Rishwat Sitaani & Bad Unwaani). ▼

Definitions

Corruption is any act where someone's right is usurped and benefit is given to an undeserving person. Bribery is a specific form of corruption where a gift or payment is given to an authority figure for wrongful gain.

Quranic Prohibition

"Do not consume one another's wealth unjustly, nor offer it as bribery to the rulers so that you may knowingly consume a portion of people's wealth in sin" (Al-Baqarah 2:188).

Prophetic Prohibition

The Prophet ﷺ cursed both the giver and taker of bribes equally (Abu Dawood: 3580). He also forbade accepting gifts acquired during official duty. He always appointed the most qualified person to every office.

Harms

Bribery and corruption spread despair, deviation, oppression and injustice. Social peace is destroyed. At the national level, power falls into unqualified hands. Forms include betrayal, dishonesty, embezzlement and manipulation.

The Islamic Solution

The merit-based system of the Prophet ﷺ and Khulafa-e-Rashideen (RA) — free from bribery, nepotism and dishonesty — enabled the State of Madinah to expand from Africa to Europe in a remarkably short period. Islam's emphasis on selflessness, equality and brotherhood eliminates bribery at its root.

Write a detailed note on Foul Language (Fahsh Goi). ▼

Definition

Foul language (Fahsh Goi) means vulgar speech, swearing and immoral conversation. It is among the sins of the tongue. Importantly, it is not limited to swearing — it includes every word or sentence that causes hurt, distress or pain to anyone in any way.

Prophetic Teaching

The Prophet ﷺ said: "A complete Muslim is one from whose tongue and hand other Muslims are safe" (Bukhari: 10). This sets the standard — a Muslim's speech must never harm another person.

Quranic Guidance

Allah commands: "And do not approach immoralities — what is apparent of them and what is concealed" (Al-An'am 6:151). Islam also forbids calling others by bad names or offensive titles, and discourages useless arguments and debates.

Social Impact

Foul language destroys relationships, creates enmity, breaks hearts and poisons the social environment. A society where tongues are guarded is a society of peace, respect and mutual love.
