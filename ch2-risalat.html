Risalat — Prophethood of Muhammad ﷺ

Chapter 2(B) — Faith (Imaniyat) · Characteristics of the Prophethood of Muhammad ﷺ · Textbook pp. 20–25

بِسْمِ اللهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيْمِ "In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful."

🎯 Learning Outcomes

After studying this lesson, students will be able to:

  1. Understand the meaning and concept of the belief in Risalat (Prophethood).
  2. Know the definition, importance, types, and various forms of Wahi (Revelation).
  3. Gain understanding of the characteristics and miracles of the Prophethood of Muhammad ﷺ.
  4. Examine the rational and textual proofs of the belief in Khatm-e-Nabuwwat (Finality of Prophethood).
  5. Demonstrate complete faith, love, obedience, following, and reverence for the Prophet ﷺ.
  6. Understand the requirements of Khatm-e-Nabuwwat, fulfill the duty of inviting to and protecting the Deen, and consider the denier of Khatm-e-Nabuwwat as outside the fold of Islam.

📖 What is Risalat (Prophethood)?

Linguistic Meaning: Risalat (رسالت) literally means "to deliver a message".

Technical Meaning: The belief that Allah sent approximately 124,000 Prophets (Anbiya, AS) for the guidance of humanity.

  • The first Prophet was Hazrat Adam (AS).
  • The last and final Prophet is Hazrat Muhammad ﷺ.
  • Allah conveyed His message to the Prophets through Wahi (Revelation).
· · ·

📬 Wahi — Divine Revelation

Definition of Wahi

Linguistic: Wahi (وحی) means "to gesture" or "to secretly place a thought into someone's heart."

Technical: Allah's method of sending His message to His Prophets through:

  • An angel (e.g., Jibra'eel AS)
  • Directly placing the message into the Prophet's heart
  • Speaking from behind a veil

The Two Types of Wahi

1. Wahi Matlu (وحی متلو)

Recited Revelation

Both the words and meanings are from Allah, and it is recited in its original form.

Example: The Holy Quran — every word is Allah's exact word, recited in Salah.

2. Wahi Ghair Matlu (وحی غیر متلو)

Unrecited Revelation

Only the meanings and concepts were inspired into the Prophet's ﷺ heart, and he ﷺ expressed them in his own words, actions, or both.

Example: The Ahadith — meaning from Allah, wording from the Prophet ﷺ.

Exam Distinction: Wahi Matlu = Words + Meanings from Allah → Quran (recited in worship). Wahi Ghair Matlu = Meanings from Allah, words from Prophet ﷺ → Hadith (not recited in Salah). Both are divine in origin.

The Three Forms of Wahi (Surah Ash-Shura 42:51)

💭

1. Heart Inspiration

Allah places a message into the Prophet's heart in wakefulness or dream.

🗣️

2. Behind a Veil

Allah speaks from behind a curtain, as with Musa (AS) at Mount Toor.

🪽

3. Through an Angel

Allah sends Jibra'eel (AS) to deliver the message.

⭐ Characteristics of the Prophethood of Muhammad ﷺ

Allah gathered the qualities of all previous Prophets in the person of Muhammad ﷺ and granted him countless unique distinctions not given to any Prophet before him. These include: Khatm-e-Nabuwwat, the Preserved Book, Intercession (Shafa'at), the Pool of Kawthar, and the Praised Station (Maqam-e-Mahmood).

Faith in Risalat is not complete until one acknowledges the unique, distinguished, and final status of the Prophethood of Muhammad ﷺ.

1. Universality (Aalmgeeriyat)

Previous Prophets were sent for specific regions or nations. The Prophethood of Muhammad ﷺ is for the entire world and all humanity until the Day of Judgment.

قُلْ يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ إِنِّي رَسُولُ اللّٰهِ إِلَيْكُمْ جَمِيعًا "Say: O Mankind! Indeed, I am the Messenger of Allah to you all." Surah Al-A'raf 7:158

2. Eternity (Abdiyyat)

Following the Prophet ﷺ is obligatory until the Day of Judgment. His Shariah has abrogated all previous religious laws.

وَمَنْ يَّبْتَغِ غَيْرَ الْإِسْلَامِ دِينًا فَلَنْ يُّقْبَلَ مِنْهُ "Whoever seeks a religion other than Islam — it will never be accepted from him." Surah Aal-e-Imran 3:85

3. Perfection (Kaamiliyat)

The chain of Prophethood that began with Adam (AS) reached its perfection and completion with the arrival of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. He was granted the complete and perfected religion.

اَلْيَوْمَ أَكْمَلْتُ لَكُمْ دِينَكُمْ وَأَتْمَمْتُ عَلَيْكُمْ نِعْمَتِي وَرَضِيتُ لَكُمُ الْإِسْلَامَ دِينًا "This day I have perfected for you your religion, completed My favor upon you, and have approved for you Islam as your religion." Surah Al-Ma'idah 5:3

4. The Preserved Book (Mahfooz Kitab)

The books revealed to previous Prophets have either been completely lost or altered to such an extent that distinguishing truth from falsehood is impossible. In contrast, the Quran revealed to the Prophet ﷺ is preserved in its original form to this day. Allah Himself has taken the responsibility of its protection.

إِنَّا نَحْنُ نَزَّلْنَا الذِّكْرَ وَإِنَّا لَهُ لَحَافِظُونَ "Indeed, it is We Who sent down the Reminder (Quran), and indeed, We will be its Guardian." Surah Al-Hijr 15:9

5. The Preserved Biography (Mahfooz Seerat)

Unlike the biographies of previous Prophets, which have not been preserved in their original form, the Sunnah and life of the Prophet ﷺ are fully preserved. His sayings, actions, and life events are available in authentic form, from which humanity will continue to receive guidance until the Day of Judgment.

A unique distinction of the Prophethood of Muhammad ﷺ is that his blessed life is the best example for every class of humanity.

لَقَدْ كَانَ لَكُمْ فِي رَسُولِ اللّٰهِ أُسْوَةٌ حَسَنَةٌ "Indeed, in the Messenger of Allah ﷺ you have an excellent example." Surah Al-Ahzab 33:21

6. Miracles (Mu'jizaat)

Allah granted the Prophet ﷺ numerous miracles as proof of his Prophethood. The Quran and Hadith mention countless miracles, including:

  • 🌙 Splitting of the Moon (Shaqq-ul-Qamar)
  • 🌌 Isra & Mi'raj (Night Journey & Ascension)
  • 💧 Water flowing from his blessed fingers
  • 🌳 Trees and stones speaking to him
  • 🐪 Animals conversing with him
  • 🌸 Fragrance emanating from his blessed body
  • 🪽 Angels assisting him and the Companions in battle
  • 🍞 Abundance and blessings in food and drink
  • 💊 His blessed saliva becoming a cure
  • 📝 Jawami' al-Kalim (comprehensive speech — few words, vast meanings)

7. Khatm-e-Nabuwwat — The Finality of Prophethood

Khatm-e-Nabuwwat means that the chain of Prophethood that began with Adam (AS) ended with Muhammad ﷺ. He is the last link in the chain of Prophets. A preserved, comprehensive, and eternal Book was revealed to him, and he was granted a complete and perfect Shariah that abrogated all previous laws.

The Prophet ﷺ is "Khatam-un-Nabiyyeen" — the Seal of the Prophets. Allah declared this explicitly in the Quran:

مَا كَانَ مُحَمَّدٌ أَبَا أَحَدٍ مِّن رِّجَالِكُمْ وَلٰكِن رَّسُولَ اللّٰهِ وَخَاتَمَ النَّبِيِّينَ "Muhammad ﷺ is not the father of any of your men, but he is the Messenger of Allah and the Seal of the Prophets." Surah Al-Ahzab 33:40

Hadith Proofs:

أَنَا خَاتَمُ النَّبِيِّينَ لَا نَبِيَّ بَعْدِي "I am the last of the Prophets; there is no Prophet after me." Sahih Bukhari: 3535

"Among the Children of Israel, when one Prophet passed away, another would succeed him. But there is no Prophet after me." Sahih Bukhari: 3455

📜 Historical Consensus on Khatm-e-Nabuwwat

The belief in Khatm-e-Nabuwwat is backed by the unanimous consensus (Ijma') of the Companions (RA) and the entire Muslim Ummah. The Companions were absolutely agreed that the question of any Prophet after Muhammad ﷺ simply does not arise.

The Battle of Yamama — Defense of Khatm-e-Nabuwwat

After the Prophet's ﷺ passing, Musaylimah the Liar (Kazzab) claimed Prophethood. The Companions (RA) fought the Battle of Yamama against him, sacrificing their lives in defense of this belief. Hundreds of Companions were martyred, including a large number of Huffaz (memorizers of the Quran).

Other false claimants during the Companions' era included Aswad Ansi, Sajah, and Tulayha. The Companions fought all of them. (Tulayha later repented and re-embraced Islam.)

Since the era of the Companions, the unanimous verdict of the Ummah has been: anyone who claims Prophethood after Muhammad ﷺ is a liar (Kazzab) and is outside the fold of Islam.

🇵🇰 Constitutional Recognition

The Constitution of Pakistan (1973) defines a Muslim as: "One who believes in the Oneness of Allah and accepts Muhammad ﷺ as the last Prophet of Allah."

Therefore, anyone who accepts any claimant to Prophethood after the Prophet ﷺ, in any capacity or title, is a liar and outside the circle of Islam.

🧠 Rational Proofs of Khatm-e-Nabuwwat

Beyond the Quranic and Hadith evidence, there are also logical/rational proofs:

  • The Deen is complete (5:3). When the religion is perfected, there is no rational need for another Prophet or another religion.
  • The Quran is preserved. Previous Prophets were needed partly because their books were lost or corrupted. Since the Quran is divinely protected, no new Prophet is needed to restore it.
  • The Seerat is preserved. The Prophet's ﷺ life example is available in full detail for all of humanity until the Day of Judgment.
Practical Requirement: Believing the Prophet ﷺ to be Allah's true and final Prophet demands that we act upon the Deen he brought and convey it to others. The Quran and the Prophet's ﷺ Uswa-e-Hasana are sufficient for the guidance of all humanity in every field of life.

Q2. Short Questions

(i) Explain the meaning and concept of Wahi. ▼

Linguistically, Wahi means "to gesture" or "to secretly place a thought in someone's heart." Technically, it is Allah's method of sending His message to His Prophets — through an angel, direct inspiration into the heart, or speaking from behind a veil. Wahi has two types: Wahi Matlu (Quran — words and meanings from Allah) and Wahi Ghair Matlu (Hadith — meanings from Allah, words from the Prophet ﷺ).

(ii) Write briefly on the preservation of the Prophet's ﷺ biography. ▼

Unlike the biographies of previous Prophets, the life of Muhammad ﷺ is preserved in complete, authentic detail. His sayings, actions, approvals, and attributes are recorded in the Ahadith and Seerah literature. Allah Himself arranged for this preservation so that humanity can follow his Uswa-e-Hasana until the Day of Judgment. Allah says: "Indeed, in the Messenger of Allah you have an excellent example." (33:21)

Q3. Detailed Question

Write a detailed note on the belief in Khatm-e-Nabuwwat. ▼

Meaning: Khatm-e-Nabuwwat means the chain of Prophethood from Adam (AS) ended with Muhammad ﷺ. He is the last and final Prophet. He was given a complete, preserved, and eternal Book and Shariah that abrogated all previous laws.

Quranic Proof: "Muhammad is not the father of any of your men, but he is the Messenger of Allah and the Seal of the Prophets." (33:40)

Hadith Proofs: The Prophet ﷺ said: "I am the last of the Prophets; there is no Prophet after me" (Bukhari: 3535). He also said: "Among the Children of Israel, when one Prophet died, another succeeded him. But there is no Prophet after me" (Bukhari: 3455).

Historical Consensus: The Companions (RA) and the entire Ummah are unanimous. During the Battle of Yamama, hundreds of Companions were martyred fighting Musaylimah the Liar. Other false claimants (Aswad Ansi, Sajah, Tulayha) were also fought. The Ummah's verdict: anyone claiming Prophethood after Muhammad ﷺ is a liar and outside Islam.

Rational Proofs: The Deen is complete (5:3), the Quran is preserved (15:9), and the Seerat is preserved (33:21). When the religion is perfected and its sources are protected, there is no logical need for another Prophet.

Constitutional Recognition: The Constitution of Pakistan (1973) defines a Muslim as one who accepts Muhammad ﷺ as the last Prophet.

The belief in Khatm-e-Nabuwwat demands that we act upon the Deen the Prophet ﷺ brought and convey it to others. The Quran and Uswa-e-Hasana are sufficient for all of humanity.
