Chapter 3(C): The Prophet ﷺ as an Ideal Commander-in-Chief

Strategy, courage, consultation, mercy & the Islamic concept of Jihad vs. terrorism

The Perfect Military Leader

The blessed life of the Prophet ﷺ is a complete model for every member of human society — whether student, teacher, merchant, labourer, ruler or commander. Among his many qualities, he ﷺ possessed all the attributes of a successful general and commander-in-chief.

As a commander, the Prophet ﷺ achieved extraordinary victories with extremely limited manpower. His military strategy was unmatched and unparalleled.

🗡️ Secrets of the Prophet's ﷺ Victories

  • Iman and Taqwa (faith and God-consciousness)
  • Shuja'at and Bahaduri (courage and bravery)
  • Unmatched mental and psychological strength
  • Harbi maharat (military expertise)
• • •

Key Military Qualities

⚔️ Unmatched Courage

The Prophet ﷺ was the epitome of bravery. In the Battle of Uhud and the Battle of Hunain, despite the most severe circumstances, he ﷺ stood firm on the battlefield and challenged the enemy.

🧠 Master Strategist

He ﷺ was an exceptional planner. With an extremely small force and minimal resources, he ﷺ inflicted a decisive defeat on the enemy at the Battle of Badr. At the Battle of Khandaq (Trench), despite scarcity of resources, he ﷺ not only faced internal and external enemies but forced them to retreat in failure.

🤫 Guardian of Military Secrets

The Prophet ﷺ protected military secrets so effectively that no one would get even a whisper of his intentions and plans. At the Conquest of Makkah (Fath-e-Makkah), he ﷺ reached Makkah before the enemy even received any intelligence.

👁️ Talent Recognition

He ﷺ was such a judge of talent that he assigned duties to the Sahaba (RA) according to their individual abilities — which they carried out excellently.

• • •

Duties of a Commander

A general and commander's duty is to plan with foresight, wisdom and insight. Specifically, he must:

  1. Be skilled in the art of warfare.
  2. Appoint the most capable officers.
  3. Ensure discipline and order in the army.
  4. Encourage the soldiers.
  5. Make a sympathetic relationship with troops his priority.
  6. Equip the army with the latest weapons and technology.
  7. Arrange proper training.
  8. Keep the army alert, agile and ready.
  9. Demonstrate spiritual courage and bravery on the battlefield.

📊 Military Record

The Prophet ﷺ personally led the Islamic army in approximately 28 Ghazwat (battles) during his blessed life.

• • •

Preference for Peace

The Prophet ﷺ avoided fighting as much as possible and always sought the path of peace. After the Hijrah, the Meezaq-e-Madinah was a living proof of his ﷺ love for peace.

When the disbelievers initiated the wars, the Prophet ﷺ defended himself and avoided aggressive warfare. When the permission for Jihad was revealed, efforts were intensified for the glory of Islam — with no room for personal interest.

وَإِنْ جَنَحُوا لِلسَّلْمِ فَاجْنَحْ لَهَا وَتَوَكَّلْ عَلَى اللّٰهِ
"And if they (the disbelievers) incline towards peace, then you also incline towards it, and put your trust in Allah."
— Surah Al-Anfal 8:61

🕊️ Sulh-e-Hudaibiyah — The Ultimate Example

At the Treaty of Hudaibiyah, 1,400 devoted Sahaba (RA) were with the Prophet ﷺ and had already taken the pledge of death (Bai'at-e-Rizwan). Yet, instead of fighting, the Prophet ﷺ accepted the enemy's conditions and made peace.

Summary of his ﷺ teaching: Avoid fighting as much as possible. But if fighting becomes necessary, then stand firm, be patient, and face the enemy with courage and bravery.

"دشمن کے ساتھ جنگ کی خواہش اور تمنا دل میں نہ رکھا کرو بلکہ اللہ تعالیٰ سے امن و عافیت کی دعا کیا کرو البتہ جب دشمن سے مقابلہ ہو ہی جائے تو پھر صبر و استقامت کا مظاہرہ کرو، یاد رکھو کہ جنت تلواروں کے سائے تلے ہے۔"
"Do not wish to face the enemy and pray to Allah for peace and well-being. However, if you do face the enemy, then show patience and steadfastness. Remember: Paradise lies under the shade of swords."
— Sahih Bukhari: 2966
• • •

Consultation (Mashwarah) in War

Whenever the Prophet ﷺ faced a military situation, he ﷺ would:

  • Show patience, courage and bravery.
  • Consult the Sahaba (RA), listen to their opinions, and take appropriate action based on their counsel.

⚔️ Battle of Uhud

The Prophet ﷺ acted on the advice of the young Sahaba (RA) and decided to go out of Madinah and fight in the open field — rather than defending from within the city.

🏗️ Battle of Khandaq

The Prophet ﷺ gave importance to the opinion of Salman Farsi (RA) and decided to dig a trench around the city and defend from within — a strategy unknown to the Arabs at the time.

• • •

Etiquette of War (Aadab-e-Jihad)

The Prophet ﷺ issued strict instructions to the Sahaba (RA) regarding the conduct of war:

  • 🚫 Do not kill the elderly, young children, or women.
  • 🚫 Do not mistreat prisoners of war.
  • 🚫 Do not mutilate the bodies of the slain.
  • 🚫 Do not block roads and paths.
  • 🚫 Do not burn any enemy in fire.
  • 🚫 Do not fight those not involved in the war.
  • 🚫 Do not launch night attacks on the enemy.
  • 🚫 Do not kill religious leaders of other faiths.
  • 🚫 Do not damage peaceful places of worship of other religions.
  • 🚫 Do not cut down green trees.
• • •

Treatment of Prisoners & the Conquered

🤲 Battle of Badr

The prisoners of Badr were the very people who had tortured and forced the Prophet ﷺ and his companions into exile. Instead of taking revenge, the Prophet ﷺ handed them over to the Sahaba (RA) with orders to treat them with kindness and gentleness.

🕋 Conquest of Makkah

At the Fath-e-Makkah, the Prophet ﷺ declared a general amnesty for his bitterest enemies — without taking any revenge. This remains one of the greatest acts of mercy in human history.

• • •

Benefits of Following the Prophet's ﷺ Military Model

  • ✅ Following the Prophet's ﷺ example makes victory possible.
  • ✅ Maximum objectives are achieved with minimum damage.
  • ✅ The defence of the Islamic state and society is strengthened.
  • ✅ Encouraging soldiers boosts the army's morale.
  • ✅ Implementing the Prophet's ﷺ Seerat as a commander promotes peace and security in Islamic society.
• • •

Jihad vs. Terrorism — A Clear Distinction

☪️ Jihad fi Sabeelillah

Study of the Prophet's ﷺ military activities reveals that the fundamental purpose of all his Jihad was:

  • Defence of the weak
  • Help for the oppressed
  • Spread of Islam
  • I'la-e-Kalimatullah (supremacy of the word of Allah)

So that the creation of Allah could live a peaceful life under the guidance of Allah.

🚫 Terrorism

Actions motivated by personal gain or interests, spreading fear among people, or seizing their wealth and property fall under the category of terrorism.

We must condemn such actions and promote the teachings of the Prophet's ﷺ Seerat so that we can play our role in the peace, security and stability of society.

Question 3: Detailed Answer

Write a detailed note on the qualities and teachings of the Prophet ﷺ as a commander-in-chief. ▼

Introduction

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ is a complete model for every sphere of life, including military leadership. As a commander-in-chief, he ﷺ achieved extraordinary victories with minimal forces, setting an unmatched standard of strategy, courage, mercy and discipline.

1. Military Qualities

  • Courage: Stood firm at Uhud and Hunain in the most dire circumstances.
  • Strategy: Defeated a vastly superior force at Badr; devised the trench strategy at Khandaq.
  • Secrecy: Reached Makkah before the enemy knew at Fath-e-Makkah.
  • Talent recognition: Assigned roles based on individual abilities of the Sahaba (RA).

2. Preference for Peace

The Prophet ﷺ always preferred peace over war. The Meezaq-e-Madinah was proof of his peace-loving nature. When the disbelievers initiated hostilities, he defended but never attacked first. At Hudaibiyah, with 1,400 battle-ready Sahaba, he chose peace over confrontation. The Quran commands: "If they incline towards peace, then you also incline towards it" (Al-Anfal 8:61). He ﷺ taught: "Do not wish to face the enemy; pray for peace. But if you face them, be patient — Paradise is under the shade of swords" (Bukhari: 2966).

3. Consultation

The Prophet ﷺ always consulted his companions in military matters. At Uhud, he followed the young Sahaba's advice to fight outside Madinah. At Khandaq, he adopted Salman Farsi's (RA) innovative trench strategy — demonstrating that a great leader values the input of his team.

4. Etiquette of War

The Prophet ﷺ established the most humane rules of warfare in history: no killing of the elderly, children or women; no mutilation of bodies; no burning; no night attacks; no killing of religious leaders; no damaging places of worship; no cutting of green trees; no blocking of roads.

5. Treatment of Prisoners & the Conquered

At Badr, the prisoners who had tortured and exiled the Muslims were treated with kindness and gentleness. At the Conquest of Makkah, the Prophet ﷺ declared a general amnesty for his bitterest enemies — an act of mercy unparalleled in human history.

6. Jihad vs. Terrorism

The Prophet's ﷺ Jihad was solely for the defence of the weak, help for the oppressed, spread of Islam and I'la-e-Kalimatullah. Actions motivated by personal gain, spreading fear, or seizing property are terrorism — and must be condemned.

Conclusion

The Prophet ﷺ as a commander combined unmatched military genius with extraordinary mercy, strict discipline with deep consultation, and absolute courage with a fundamental love for peace. His model proves that true strength lies not in destruction but in justice, restraint and the pursuit of peace.
