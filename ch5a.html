Chapter 5(A): Huqooq-ul-Ibad — Rights of People

Rights of teachers, support staff, spouses, children & widows in light of Quran, Sunnah & Seerat

🎯 Learning Outcomes

After studying this lesson, students will be able to:

  • Understand the meaning, concept and importance of Huqooq-ul-Ibad (Rights of People).
  • Know the rights of teachers, support staff, spouses, children and widows in light of the Quran and Hadith.
  • Understand examples of upholding these rights from the Seerat-e-Nabawi ﷺ, the Ahl-e-Bait and the Sahaba (RA).
  • Assess the worldly and spiritual consequences of failing to fulfil these rights.
  • Become someone who fulfils the rights of people in practical life.
  • Understand the sensitivity of accountability for Huqooq-ul-Ibad in the Hereafter and avoid neglecting them.
• • •

What Are Huqooq-ul-Ibad?

Huqooq-ul-Ibad: The rights of Allah's servants (people). After the rights of Allah, Islam gives enormous importance to the rights of people.

⚠️ A Critical Distinction

On the Day of Judgment, Allah may forgive shortcomings in His own rights — such as deficiencies in Salah, fasting, Zakat and Hajj — out of His infinite mercy. However, Huqooq-ul-Ibad will NOT be forgiven until the person whose rights were violated personally forgives or the rights are fully repaid.

This makes the rights of people one of the most delicate matters in Islam.

• • •

1. Rights of Teachers (Asatiza)

Teachers are the benefactors of the nation. They adorn children with the jewel of knowledge. In every civilised society, teachers are respected. In the Islamic way of life, the status of teachers is extremely elevated — a teacher is regarded with the respect of a father.

إِنَّمَا بُعِثْتُ مُعَلِّمًا
"Indeed, I have been sent as a teacher."
— Sunan Ibn Majah: 229

📚 Rights of Teachers (Imam Zain-ul-Abideen RA)

Hazrat Imam Zain-ul-Abideen (RA) outlined many rights of teachers. Key ones include:

  • ✅ Respect your teacher at all times.
  • ✅ Do not talk among yourselves during class in front of the teacher.
  • ✅ If someone speaks ill of your teacher, defend them.
  • ✅ Conceal your teacher's faults and highlight their virtues.
  • ✅ If someone asks a question in the teacher's presence, do not answer before the teacher does.

Additionally, students should:

  • Serve the teacher as much as possible.
  • If the teacher scolds or reprimands, accept it with a smile and try to improve.
  • Listen to the teacher with full attention and focus.
  • Always pray for the teacher's well-being.
• • •

2. Rights of Support Staff (Mu'awin Amala)

Support Staff: Individuals who assist those in higher positions in fulfilling their responsibilities. They may not hold major offices, but their contribution is essential to the functioning of any institution or state.

In the Islamic system, both parties have responsibilities:

  • Support staff must fulfil their duties properly.
  • Those in higher positions must treat subordinates, servants and employees well, respect their dignity and give them their due rights.
  • Both will be questioned on the Day of Judgment about their responsibilities.
"نماز کا اہتمام کرنا اور اپنے ماتحتوں کے بارے میں اللہ سے ڈرتے رہنا۔"
"Take care of Salah, and fear Allah regarding your subordinates."
— Sunan Abu Dawood: 4889 (The Prophet's ﷺ last words)

These were the very last words on the Prophet's ﷺ tongue at the time of his passing — showing just how critical this matter is.

"جب تمھارا خادم تمھارے لیے کھانا لے کر آئے تو اسے اپنے ساتھ بٹھا کر کھانا کھلاؤ، اگر ساتھ نہ بھی بٹھاؤ تو اسے اپنے کھانے میں سے ضرور دو۔"
"When your servant brings you food, sit him down and eat with him. If you do not sit him down, then at least give him some of your food."
— Sunan Ibn Majah: 3289
"تمھارے یہ بھائی تمھارے خدمت گزار اور تمھارے لیے اللہ تعالیٰ کا عطیہ ہیں۔ اللہ تعالیٰ نے انھیں تمھاری سرپرستی میں دیا ہے۔ جس کے پاس اس کا کوئی ایسا بھائی ہو تو جو خود کھائے وہ اسے کھلائے، جو خود پہنے وہ اسے پہنائے، انھیں ایسے کاموں کا پابند نہ بنائے جس کی وہ طاقت نہ رکھتے ہوں۔ اگر ان پر کوئی مشکل کام ڈالو تو اس میں ان کی مدد بھی کرو۔"
"These are your brothers — your servants, and Allah's gift to you, placed under your care. Whoever has a brother under his care should feed him what he eats, clothe him what he wears, not burden him with work beyond his capacity, and if he does assign a difficult task, help him with it."
— Sahih Muslim: 4315

📋 Rights of Support Staff & Employees

  • ✅ Do not consider subordinates and employees inferior.
  • ✅ Provide financial help to employees in need.
  • ✅ Avoid hurting employees' feelings as much as possible.
  • ✅ Pay special attention to employees' needs.
  • ✅ Avoid unjust punishment and harsh scolding.
  • ✅ Arrange special allowances and bonuses on festive occasions.

3. Rights of Spouses (Zaujain)

Zaujain: Husband and wife. Both must fulfil each other's rights — without this, a peaceful life is impossible. The Shariah has given great importance to the mutual rights of spouses.

(a) Rights of the Husband

A woman who is obedient and compliant to her husband has been given great virtue. Severe warnings have been given regarding disobedience. The Prophet ﷺ said:

"اگر میں کسی کو یہ حکم کر سکتا کہ وہ کسی (غیر اللہ) کو سجدہ کرے تو میں یقیناً عورت کو حکم کرتا کہ وہ اپنے خاوند کو سجدہ کرے۔"
"If I could command anyone to prostrate to someone other than Allah, I would have commanded the woman to prostrate to her husband (as a sign of respect and honour)."
— Sunan Abu Dawood: 2140
"جس عورت نے پانچوں وقت کی نماز پڑھی، رمضان کے روزے رکھے، اپنی عزت و عصمت کی حفاظت کی اور اپنے خاوند کی فرماں برداری کی تو وہ جس دروازے سے چاہے جنت میں داخل ہو جائے۔"
"A woman who prays her five daily prayers, fasts in Ramadan, guards her chastity and obeys her husband — she may enter Paradise from whichever door she wishes."
— Musnad Ahmad: 1661

A wife should consider her husband superior to herself, remain loyal and obedient, never fall short in seeking his pleasure and goodwill, and consider her worldly and spiritual well-being tied to his happiness.

(b) Rights of the Wife

The Quran and Hadith give equal importance to women's rights. Fulfilling the wife's rights is equally essential for the husband. Allah says:

"...وَعَاشِرُوْهُنَّ بِالْمَعْرُوْفِ ۚ فَاِنْ كَرِهْتُمُوْهُنَّ فَعَسٰٓى اَنْ تَكْرَهُوْا شَيْئًا وَّيَجْعَلَ اللّٰهُ فِيْهِ خَيْرًا كَثِيْرًا"
"...And live with them in kindness. And if you dislike them, perhaps you dislike something in which Allah has placed much good."
— Surah An-Nisa 4:19
"مومنین میں سے کامل ترین ایمان اس شخص کا ہے جو ان میں سے بہت زیادہ خوش اخلاق ہو، اور تم میں بہتر وہ شخص ہے جو اپنی عورتوں کے حق میں بہتر ہے۔"
"The most complete in faith among the believers is the one with the best character, and the best among you is the one who is best to his women."
— Jami' Tirmidhi: 1162

A husband must:

  • Win his wife's heart and express love.
  • Praise and appreciate her.
  • Take excellent care of her — ignoring or belittling a wife has devastating effects on the family.
  • Consider her a blessing from Allah and value her.
  • If she makes a mistake, overlook it and try to correct her with patience, tolerance and wisdom.
  • Fulfil her needs to the best of his ability and ensure her comfort.

4. Rights of Children (Aulad)

Children are a blessing from Allah. They bring joy and brightness to life. Whether a son or a daughter, both are sources of happiness. The Prophet ﷺ loved his children deeply and declared his daughter Hazrat Fatimah (RA) the leader of the women of Paradise.

"اللہ سے ڈرو اور اپنی اولاد کے درمیان عدل کرو۔"
"Fear Allah and be just among your children."
— Sahih Bukhari: 2587

👶 Rights of Children

  • ✅ Good name: The child's first right is a good name — one with a good meaning or noble association.
  • ✅ Education and upbringing: The best arrangement for the child's education, including both spiritual and physical knowledge.
  • ✅ Life skills and good habits: Teaching children the etiquette of living and good manners.
  • ✅ Overlooking mistakes: Forgiving children's errors as much as possible, with gentle reprimand only where necessary.
  • ✅ Avoiding unjust anger: Refraining from unnecessary scolding and harsh treatment.
  • ✅ Justice among children: Treating all children fairly and equally.
  • ✅ Extra attention: If a child needs more attention, they should be given it.
• • •

5. Rights of Widows (Biwa)

Biwa (Widow): A woman whose husband has passed away.

⚠️ Pre-Islamic Treatment

Before Islam, widows were treated with extreme cruelty:

  • 🚫 They were considered property and inheritance.
  • 🚫 In some religions, they were denied the right to live.
  • 🚫 They were not allowed to remarry.

✨ Islam's Revolution

Islam gave widows the right to remarry and placed special emphasis on treating them with kindness. The Prophet ﷺ himself set the greatest example — most of the Mothers of the Ummah (his wives) were widows. He ﷺ not only married them but established the best examples of social conduct with them.

"بیوہ اور مسکین کے لیے دوڑ دھوپ کرنے والا اس شخص کی طرح ہے جو اللہ تعالیٰ کی راہ میں جہاد کرتا ہے یا اس شخص کی طرح ہے جو دن کو روزہ رکھتا ہے اور رات کو قیام کرتا ہے۔"
"The one who strives for the widow and the poor is like the one who fights in the path of Allah, or like the one who fasts by day and prays by night."
— Sahih Bukhari: 5353
"رسول اللہ ﷺ اس بات میں کوئی عار محسوس نہیں فرماتے تھے کہ کسی بیوہ یا مسکین کے ساتھ چل کر جائیں اور ان کی ضرورت پوری کر دیں۔"
"The Messenger of Allah ﷺ felt no shame in walking alongside a widow or a poor person and fulfilling their needs."
— Sunan Nasai: 1414

📋 Rights of Widows in Islam

  • ✅ Iddah (waiting period) limited to 4 months and 10 days (if not pregnant) — replacing permanent mourning. If pregnant, until delivery.
  • ✅ Inheritance share from the husband's estate is guaranteed.
  • ✅ Usurping a widow's wealth is declared a grave crime.
  • ✅ The concept of bad omens associated with widows was abolished — all matters are linked to Allah's decree.
  • ✅ The widow has the right to remarry and live according to her own choice.
  • ✅ Widows are freed from unjust coercion and fear.
  • ✅ The care and maintenance of widows is the duty of the state and responsible members of society.
• • •

Conclusion

When a person treats others well and fulfils their rights, it shows that they have received good upbringing and are a good human being. We should strive to respect and fulfil the rights of others in every possible way so that we may be successful in this world and the Hereafter.

Question 2: Short Answers

(i) How do you show respect to your teachers? ▼

Based on the teachings of Imam Zain-ul-Abideen (RA) and the Seerat-e-Nabawi ﷺ, I show respect to my teachers by: listening attentively during class, not talking among classmates in the teacher's presence, defending my teacher if someone speaks ill of them, concealing their faults and highlighting their virtues, not answering before the teacher when a question is asked, accepting reprimands with a smile and trying to improve, and always praying for their well-being.

(ii) Write two rights of the husband. ▼
  1. Obedience and compliance: The wife should obey her husband and remain loyal to him. The Prophet ﷺ said that if he could command prostration to anyone other than Allah, he would have commanded the woman to prostrate to her husband (Abu Dawood: 2140).
  2. Seeking his pleasure: The wife should never fall short in seeking her husband's pleasure and goodwill, and should consider her worldly and spiritual well-being tied to his happiness.
(iii) Write two rights of the wife. ▼
  1. Kind treatment: The husband must treat his wife with kindness and live with her honourably. Allah commands: "Live with them in kindness" (An-Nisa 4:19).
  2. Love and appreciation: The husband must express love, praise and appreciation for his wife, take excellent care of her, and never ignore or belittle her. The Prophet ﷺ said: "The best among you is the one who is best to his women" (Tirmidhi: 1162).
(iv) Write two rights of children. ▼
  1. A good name: The child's very first right is to be given a good name — one with a good meaning or noble association.
  2. Education and upbringing: Parents must provide the best arrangement for the child's education, including both spiritual and physical knowledge, and teach them the etiquette of living and good habits.
(v) Write two rights of widows. ▼
  1. Right to remarry: Islam gave widows the right to remarry and live according to their own choice, abolishing the pre-Islamic practice of permanent mourning and forced celibacy.
  2. Inheritance share: A widow's share in her deceased husband's estate is guaranteed by the Shariah, and usurping her wealth is declared a grave crime.
(vi) Write two rights of support staff. ▼
  1. Respect and dignity: Subordinates and employees must not be considered inferior. Their dignity and self-respect must be protected at all times.
  2. Fair compensation and care: Employees' needs should be given special attention, and on festive occasions, special allowances and bonuses should be arranged for them.
