Chapter 3(A): The Prophet ﷺ as an Ideal Family Head

The perfect model of love, justice, respect and training within the family

🎯 Learning Outcomes

After studying this lesson, students will be able to:

  • Understand the Prophet's ﷺ exemplary treatment of children, wives, elders and extended family.
  • Learn practical guidance for a family head from the Seerat-e-Tayyibah.
  • Appreciate the importance of Silah Rahmi (maintaining kinship ties) in Islam.
  • Apply the Prophetic model to strengthen their own family relationships.
• • •

Training & Nurturing Children

📖 Advice to Abdullah bin Abbas (RA)

The Prophet ﷺ advised his young cousin Abdullah bin Abbas (RA):

"Seek help only from Allah."

This simple yet profound teaching instilled Tawakkul (reliance on Allah) in a child from an early age.

🍽️ Advice to Umar bin Abi Salamah (RA)

The Prophet ﷺ taught his young stepson Umar bin Abi Salamah (RA) the etiquette of eating:

"Say Bismillah before eating."

This shows how the Prophet ﷺ took personal interest in the tarbiyah (upbringing) of every child in his household, teaching them daily manners rooted in faith.

• • •

Love for Grandchildren & Children

🚫 The Sadaqah Date Incident

Once, a young grandchild picked up a date from the pile of Sadaqah (charity) dates and put it in his mouth. The Prophet ﷺ immediately removed it from the child's mouth and said:

"Do you not know that we, the Ahl-e-Bait, do not eat Sadaqah?"

This incident teaches that the Prophet ﷺ maintained the highest standards of halal and haram even for the youngest members of his family — no compromise, even for a single date.

💕 Love for Daughters

Despite the immense responsibilities of Prophethood, the Prophet ﷺ showed extraordinary love and care for his daughters:

  • He would visit them even after their marriages.
  • When Sayyidah Fatimah (RA) came to visit him, he would stand up to greet her, kiss her, and offer her his own seat. When he visited her, she would do the same for him. (Jami' Tirmidhi: 3872)
  • He granted amnesty to the husband of Sayyidah Zainab (RA) on her intercession.
  • During the Battle of Badr, he left Sayyidah Ruqayyah's (RA) husband, Uthman (RA), in Madinah to care for her during her illness. Upon return, the Prophet ﷺ not only counted Uthman among the Badri companions but also gave him a share of the spoils of war.

👶 Adopted Son & Grandchildren

  • Zaid bin Harithah (RA) — the Prophet's ﷺ adopted son — was treated with such love and affection that when his biological parents came to take him back, he refused to leave the Prophet ﷺ.
  • Hasan and Husain (RA) grew up under the Prophet's ﷺ direct care and upbringing. He showered them with complete love and tenderness.
  • His granddaughter Imamah bint Zainab (RA) would climb into his lap even during Salah, and the Prophet ﷺ would gently carry her — showing that mercy for children is never a distraction from worship but a part of it.
• • •

Respect for Elders & Relatives

The Prophet ﷺ showed boundless respect for his uncles, aunts and elder relatives:

"Honouring an elderly Muslim is part of honouring Allah."
— Sunan Abu Dawood: 4843

😢 Aam-ul-Huzn (Year of Sorrow)

The Prophet ﷺ declared the year of his uncle Abu Talib's death as "Aam-ul-Huzn" (the Year of Sorrow). Even long after his passing, the Prophet ﷺ would frequently recall Abu Talib's kindness and support.

⚔️ Martyrdom of Hamza (RA)

At the Battle of Uhud, when the Prophet ﷺ saw his aunt Sayyidah Safiyyah (RA) weeping over the martyrdom of her brother Hamza (RA), tears flowed from his own eyes as well. He consoled her and advised patience (Sabr).

🤝 Abu Bakr (RA) & Abu Quhafah (RA)

When Abu Bakr Siddiq (RA) brought his elderly father Abu Quhafah (RA) to the Prophet ﷺ, the Prophet ﷺ said:

"Why did you trouble him? You should have left him at home — I would have come to him myself."

This is the ultimate model of respect for the elderly: the leader of the entire Ummah offering to walk to an old man's house rather than making him travel.

👩 Foster Sister — Sayyidah Shaima (RA)

Sayyidah Shaima (RA), the daughter of Halimah Sa'diyyah (RA) and the Prophet's ﷺ foster sister, came to visit him. The Prophet ﷺ:

  • Showed her the utmost honour.
  • Spread his own shawl for her to sit on.
  • Treated her with great respect and courtesy.
  • On her intercession, released the prisoners of war from her tribe.

Treatment of Wives

خَيْرُكُمْ خَيْرُكُمْ لِاَهْلِهٖ وَاَنَا خَيْرُكُمْ لِاَهْلِیْ
"The best of you is the one who is best to his family, and I am the best of you to my family."
— Jami' Tirmidhi: 3895

💍 The Prophet's ﷺ Conduct with His Wives

  • Daily visits: He would visit every wife's house each day, inquire about her needs, and fulfil them.
  • Perfect justice: He always maintained complete equality among all his wives.
  • Travel: When going on a journey, he would draw lots among his wives and take the one whose name was drawn.
  • Emotional care: He consoled them, showed affection, and treated them with unmatched kindness and gentleness.
  • Equal provision: He fulfilled the needs of all wives in an equitable manner.
• • •

Silah Rahmi — Maintaining Kinship Ties

The life of the Prophet ﷺ teaches us that we must honour the rights of every family relationship. This strengthens family bonds and earns the reward of Silah Rahmi (maintaining kinship ties):

مَنْ سَرَّهٗ اَنْ یُّبْسَطَ لَهٗ فِیْ رِزْقِهٖ، وَاَنْ یُّنْسَاَ لَهٗ فِیْ اٰثَرِهٖ فَلْیَصِلْ رَحِمَهٗ
"Whoever loves that his provision be expanded and his life be blessed, let him maintain ties of kinship."
— Sahih Bukhari: 5985

📌 Key Takeaway

By following this aspect of the Seerat-e-Nabawi ﷺ, we should respect and strengthen family relationships so that our family system becomes stable and we attain the benefits of both this world and the Hereafter.

Question 2: Short Answers

(i) How did the Prophet ﷺ train Abdullah bin Abbas (RA)? ▼

The Prophet ﷺ gave Abdullah bin Abbas (RA) — his young cousin — a foundational lesson in Tawakkul (reliance on Allah). He advised him to "seek help only from Allah". This simple but profound teaching trained the child to turn to Allah alone in every matter of life, building a strong spiritual foundation from an early age. This shows the Prophet's ﷺ method of tarbiyah: giving age-appropriate, practical guidance rooted in Tawheed.

(ii) Write two etiquettes of eating in light of Prophetic guidance. ▼
  1. Say Bismillah before eating — The Prophet ﷺ taught his young stepson Umar bin Abi Salamah (RA) to always begin eating in the name of Allah.
  2. Eat only Halal — The Prophet ﷺ immediately removed a Sadaqah date from his grandchild's mouth, teaching that Ahl-e-Bait do not consume charity. This instils the principle that food must be from lawful sources.
(iii) What practical guidance does the Seerat provide for a family head? ▼

The Seerat-e-Nabawi ﷺ provides the following practical guidance for a family head:

  • Training children in faith and daily manners from a young age (e.g. Bismillah, Tawakkul).
  • Treating all family members with love and justice — wives, children, elders, and extended relatives.
  • Respecting elders — visiting them rather than making them travel (Abu Quhafah incident).
  • Maintaining equality among wives in time, attention, and provision.
  • Showing emotional care — consoling in grief (Safiyyah at Uhud), expressing love openly (Fatimah).
  • Upholding Silah Rahmi — maintaining kinship ties for worldly blessings and Hereafter rewards.
(iv) Write one incident of the Prophet's ﷺ love for his children. ▼

The visit of Sayyidah Fatimah (RA): Sayyidah Aishah (RA) narrates that whenever Sayyidah Fatimah (RA) came to visit the Prophet ﷺ, he would stand up to greet her, kiss her, and seat her in his own place. And when the Prophet ﷺ visited her, she would stand up, kiss him, and offer him her seat. (Jami' Tirmidhi: 3872)

This beautiful mutual exchange of love and respect between father and daughter is a timeless model for family relationships.

(v) How did the Prophet ﷺ treat Sayyidah Shaima (RA)? ▼

Sayyidah Shaima (RA) was the daughter of Halimah Sa'diyyah (RA) and the Prophet's ﷺ foster sister. When she came to visit him, the Prophet ﷺ:

  • Showed her the utmost honour and respect.
  • Spread his own shawl on the ground for her to sit on.
  • Treated her with great courtesy and affection.
  • On her intercession, released the prisoners of war from her tribe.

This demonstrates the Prophet's ﷺ deep sense of gratitude and loyalty towards even his foster family.

Question 3: Detailed Answer

Write a comprehensive note on the ideal character of the Prophet ﷺ as a family head. ▼

Introduction

The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ is the perfect role model for every aspect of human life, including the role of a family head. His conduct with children, wives, elders, relatives and even foster family members sets an unmatched standard of love, justice, respect and training.

1. Training of Children

The Prophet ﷺ took personal interest in the tarbiyah of every child in his household. He taught young Abdullah bin Abbas (RA) to seek help only from Allah, building Tawakkul from childhood. He taught Umar bin Abi Salamah (RA) to say Bismillah before eating — instilling daily Islamic manners. When a grandchild picked up a Sadaqah date, the Prophet ﷺ immediately removed it, teaching the boundary of halal and haram even to the youngest.

2. Love for Daughters

In an era when daughters were considered a burden, the Prophet ﷺ demonstrated extraordinary love for his daughters. He would stand up, kiss, and offer his seat to Sayyidah Fatimah (RA) (Tirmidhi: 3872). He granted amnesty to Zainab's husband on her request. He left Uthman (RA) in Madinah to care for the ailing Ruqayyah (RA) during Badr and still counted him as a Badri companion with a share of spoils.

3. Care for Grandchildren & Adopted Children

Hasan and Husain (RA) were raised under the Prophet's ﷺ direct care. His granddaughter Imamah bint Zainab (RA) would sit in his lap during Salah — and he carried her with gentleness. His adopted son Zaid bin Harithah (RA) was treated with such love that he refused to return to his biological parents.

4. Respect for Elders & Relatives

The Prophet ﷺ declared the year of Abu Talib's death as "Aam-ul-Huzn" and frequently recalled his kindness. He wept alongside his aunt Safiyyah (RA) at Hamza's martyrdom and counselled patience. When Abu Bakr (RA) brought his elderly father Abu Quhafah, the Prophet ﷺ said "I would have come to him myself". He spread his shawl for his foster sister Shaima (RA) and released her tribe's prisoners on her intercession. He taught: "Honouring an elderly Muslim is part of honouring Allah" (Abu Dawood: 4843).

5. Treatment of Wives

The Prophet ﷺ declared: "The best of you is the best to his family, and I am the best of you to my family" (Tirmidhi: 3895). He visited every wife daily, enquired about their needs, maintained perfect justice, drew lots for travel companionship, and fulfilled all their needs equitably.

6. Silah Rahmi (Kinship Ties)

The Prophet ﷺ taught: "Whoever loves that his provision be expanded and his life be blessed, let him maintain ties of kinship" (Bukhari: 5985). His entire life was a living example of this principle — honouring every relative, near and far, biological and foster.

Conclusion

The Prophet ﷺ as a family head combined love with discipline, justice with mercy, and authority with humility. By following his example, families can become strong, stable and blessed in this world and the Hereafter.
