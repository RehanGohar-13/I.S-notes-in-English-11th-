Heavenly Books — Kutub-e-Samawiyah

Chapter 2(D) — Faith (Imaniyat) · Belief in the Heavenly Books · Textbook pp. 29–32

بِسْمِ اللهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيْمِ "In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful."

🎯 Learning Outcomes

After studying this lesson, students will be able to:

  1. Gain a comprehensive introduction to the heavenly books and scriptures and the purpose of their revelation.
  2. Understand Wahi as an authoritative and definitive source of knowledge.
  3. Understand the textual and rational proofs for belief in the heavenly books.
  4. Extract the common teachings of all heavenly books.
  5. Recognize the importance of the Quran's teachings and act upon them.
  6. Believe firmly that the Shariahs of all previous books have been abrogated, and the Quran is the sole final heavenly book, completely protected from all forms of corruption.

📖 What are the Heavenly Books (Kutub-e-Samawiyah)?

Kutub-e-Samawiyah literally means "Heavenly Books" — the divine scriptures revealed by Allah to His Prophets (AS) for the guidance of humanity.

Allah revealed these books and scriptures so that the Prophets could:

  • Make people aware of Allah's commands
  • Warn them of His punishment
  • Give them glad tidings of His rewards

Allah commands the believers:

يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا آمِنُوا بِاللّٰهِ وَرَسُولِهِ وَالْكِتَابِ الَّذِي نَزَّلَ عَلَىٰ رَسُولِهِ وَالْكِتَابِ الَّذِي أَنزَلَ مِن قَبْلُ "O you who believe! Believe in Allah, His Messenger ﷺ, the Book He revealed to His Messenger, and the Books He revealed before." Surah An-Nisa 4:136

The Prophet ﷺ defined Iman as:

"Iman is that you believe in Allah, His angels, His books, the meeting with Him (on the Day of Judgment), His Messengers, and the resurrection after death." Sahih Muslim: 9

📚 The Four Major Heavenly Books

# Book Revealed to Status
1 Torah (تورات) Hazrat Musa (AS) Original lost/altered
2 Zaboor (زبور) Hazrat Dawud (AS) Original lost/altered
3 Injeel (انجیل) Hazrat Isa (AS) Original lost/altered
4 Quran (قرآن مجید) Hazrat Muhammad ﷺ ✅ Fully preserved
The Quran is the final heavenly book. It confirms the truth of all previous books and contains guidance for all of humanity until the Day of Judgment.

📜 The Scriptures (Suhuf)

In addition to the major books, shorter scriptures (Suhuf) containing a few commandments were also revealed to some Prophets. The Quran specifically mentions the Scriptures of Ibrahim (AS) and Musa (AS):

إِنَّ هٰذَا لَفِي الصُّحُفِ الْأُولَىٰ ۙ صُحُفِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَمُوسَىٰ "Indeed, this (teaching) is in the former scriptures — the scriptures of Ibrahim and Musa." Surah Al-A'la 87:18-19
Books vs. Scriptures: Heavenly Books (Kutub) contain detailed, comprehensive divine laws. Scriptures (Suhuf) are shorter revelations containing brief commandments.

🎯 Purpose of the Heavenly Books

Humanity needed a divine guidebook to make life decisions correctly. Allah sent the heavenly books alongside the Prophets so they could serve as practical role models for implementing divine teachings.

All these books were revealed through Wahi (Revelation). Knowledge obtained through Wahi is:

  • ✅ Definitive and certain (Qat'i) — there is absolutely no doubt in it.
  • ✅ Beyond human intellect — matters that the human mind could not grasp or might err in understanding were clarified by Allah through Wahi, which is a tremendous favor upon humanity.
· · ·

🤝 Common Teachings of All Heavenly Books

All heavenly books shared common themes in faith, worship, and morality. Three fundamental beliefs were present in every single book Allah revealed:

☪️

1. Tawheed

Oneness of Allah

🕌

2. Risalat

Prophethood

⚖️

3. Akhirat

The Hereafter

Important Principle: If any religion's teachings contradict these three fundamental beliefs, it means those teachings were not from Allah but were added by people according to their own desires.

Other Shared Teachings

Beyond the three core beliefs, all heavenly books also shared these teachings:

Acts of Worship

  • 🤲 Supplication (Du'a) to Allah
  • 🙇 Prayer (Salah) with humility (Khushu & Khudu)
  • 💰 Spending in the way of Allah
  • 🌙 Fasting (prescribed for previous nations too)
  • 🐑 Sacrifice (Qurbani) — e.g., Habil and Qabil (sons of Adam AS)

Moral Teachings

  • 💬 Truthfulness in speech
  • ⚖️ Giving others their rights
  • 🙏 Living with humility
  • 🍽️ Consuming only Halal
  • 🧼 Cleanliness and purity
  • 😳 Modesty and shame (Haya)

The Universal Invitation to Ahl-e-Kitab

The Quran invites the People of the Book (Jews and Christians) to common ground:

قُلْ يَا أَهْلَ الْكِتَابِ تَعَالَوْا إِلَىٰ كَلِمَةٍ سَوَاءٍ بَيْنَنَا وَبَيْنَكُمْ أَلَّا نَعْبُدَ إِلَّا اللّٰهَ وَلَا نُشْرِكَ بِهِ شَيْئًا وَلَا يَتَّخِذَ بَعْضُنَا بَعْضًا أَرْبَابًا مِّن دُونِ اللّٰهِ فَإِن تَوَلَّوْا فَقُولُوا اشْهَدُوا بِأَنَّا مُسْلِمُونَ "Say: O People of the Book! Come to a word that is common between us and you — that we worship none but Allah, associate nothing with Him, and none of us takes others as lords besides Allah. But if they turn away, then say: Bear witness that we are Muslims (those who submit to Allah)." Surah Aal-e-Imran 3:64

👑 The Distinction of the Holy Quran (قرآن مجید کا امتیاز)

According to Islamic belief, the Quran is the final book of Allah. After its revelation, all previous heavenly books were abrogated. Yet it remains the source of guidance for all humanity until the Day of Judgment.

Key Distinctions:

  • 🔒 Perfect Preservation: The Quran exists today in the exact same form as it was revealed to the Prophet ﷺ. Not a single letter has changed, nor will it change until the Day of Judgment.
  • 🛡️ Divine Protection: Allah Himself has taken the responsibility of protecting the Quran — preserving it in the hearts of millions (Huffaz) and through other means.
  • 🏆 Proof of Finality: The Quran's miraculous preservation is itself a proof that it is the final book — previous books were not protected because they were temporary.
  • ✨ Greatest Miracle: The Quran is the Prophet's ﷺ greatest miracle. Every letter, word, verse, and teaching is miraculous.
  • 🔬 Scientific Accuracy: The facts about the universe stated in the Quran are being confirmed by modern science in this advanced era.
  • 🌍 Universal Acknowledgment: The Quran is the only book in the world whose greatness has been openly acknowledged by experts in both religious and scientific fields across every era.
Summary: The Quran is the final, preserved, miraculous, and universal book of Allah. It abrogates all previous scriptures, contains the summary of all divine teachings, and will remain the sole source of guidance until the Day of Judgment.

Q2. Short Questions

(i) What is meant by belief in the heavenly books? ▼

Belief in the heavenly books (Kutub-e-Samawiyah) means to firmly believe that Allah revealed divine scriptures to His Prophets (AS) for the guidance of humanity. This includes belief in the Torah, Zaboor, Injeel, and the Quran, as well as the shorter scriptures (Suhuf). Allah commands: "Believe in the Book He revealed to His Messenger and the Books He revealed before" (4:136).

(ii) Give a brief introduction to the heavenly books. ▼

Allah revealed four major books: Torah to Musa (AS), Zaboor to Dawud (AS), Injeel to Isa (AS), and the Quran to Muhammad ﷺ. Additionally, shorter scriptures (Suhuf) were revealed to Prophets like Ibrahim (AS) and Musa (AS). The Quran is the final book that confirms and abrogates all previous ones.

(iii) Write two common teachings of the heavenly books. ▼

1. Tawheed: All heavenly books taught the Oneness of Allah and worshipping Him alone.

2. Good Morals: Truthfulness, giving others their rights, humility, consuming halal, and living with modesty were taught in all divine scriptures.

(iv) Describe the importance of Wahi. ▼

Wahi is the definitive and certain (Qat'i) source of knowledge from Allah. Human intellect is limited and prone to error. Through Wahi, Allah clarified matters that the human mind could not grasp on its own. All heavenly books were revealed through Wahi, making their teachings absolutely reliable and doubt-free.

(v) Review the distinguishing characteristics of the Holy Quran. ▼

The Quran's key distinctions: (1) It is the final book — all previous books are abrogated. (2) It is perfectly preserved — not a single letter has changed. (3) Allah Himself guarantees its protection. (4) It is the Prophet's ﷺ greatest miracle. (5) Its scientific facts are confirmed by modern science. (6) It is universally acknowledged by scholars of every era.

Q3. Detailed Question

Write a comprehensive essay on the topic of belief in the heavenly books. ▼

Introduction: Belief in the heavenly books (Kutub-e-Samawiyah) is a fundamental pillar of Iman. Allah revealed divine scriptures to His Prophets for the guidance of humanity (4:136).

The Four Books: Torah (Musa AS), Zaboor (Dawud AS), Injeel (Isa AS), and Quran (Muhammad ﷺ). Shorter scriptures (Suhuf) were also revealed to Prophets like Ibrahim and Musa (87:18-19).

Purpose: These books were sent as divine guidebooks through Wahi — a definitive, doubt-free source of knowledge — so humanity could live according to Allah's commands.

Common Teachings: All books shared three core beliefs: Tawheed, Risalat, and Akhirat. They also shared teachings on prayer, fasting, sacrifice, truthfulness, humility, and moral conduct.

Distinction of the Quran: The Quran is the final, perfectly preserved book. Not a single letter has changed. Allah Himself protects it. It is the greatest miracle of the Prophet ﷺ, confirmed by modern science, and acknowledged by scholars of every era. All previous Shariahs have been abrogated by it.

A Muslim must believe in all heavenly books in their original form, while recognizing that the Quran is the sole surviving, uncorrupted, and final divine scripture.
