Islamic Renaissance & Obligations of Muslims

Chapter 7(B) — نظامِ اسلام کی نشاۃِ ثانیہ

بِسْمِ اللهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيْمِ "In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful."

🎯 Learning Outcomes

After studying this lesson, students will be able to:

  1. Understand the Islamic concept of overall progress, welfare, and the true meaning of Islamic Renaissance (نشاۃِ ثانیہ).
  2. Appreciate the necessity of future planning and resource management alongside complete trust in Allah (Tawakkul).
  3. Acknowledge the glorious past and scientific inventions of Muslim scholars to address current challenges faced by the Muslim Ummah.
  4. Analyze the vital roles of science, technology, economics, and self-reliance in Muslim national development.
  5. Realize economic and social responsibilities according to modern needs and play a constructive role in society.
  6. Take pride in Islamic heritage and historical achievements as inspirational models.

📖 Concept of Islamic Renaissance and Welfare

Islam guarantees success in both this world and the Hereafter. Unlike secular philosophies that separate material progress from spiritual morality, Islam teaches a balanced life where worldly advancement is linked with spiritual righteousness.

رَبَّنَا اٰتِنَا فِی الدُّنْیَا حَسَنَةً وَّ فِی الْاٰخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً "Our Lord, give us in this world that which is good and in the Hereafter that which is good." Surah Al-Baqarah 2:201

Islamic Renaissance (نظامِ اسلام کی نشاۃِ ثانیہ) refers to the revival and reunification of the Muslim Ummah under the principles of Islam to re-establish Islamic civilization’s global leadership. This revival is not merely a Muslim need, but a pressing global necessity for all of humanity.

The Islamic model of progress ensures that fulfilling the rights of Allah (Huqooq-ullah) is intrinsically linked with protecting and serving the rights of His creation (Huqooq-ul-Ibad).

📋 Strategic Planning (منصوبہ بندی)

Planning means preparing systematically for the future by optimizing available resources and acquiring necessary means according to one’s capability. Islam strictly prohibits the squandering of resources:

وَلَا تُسْرِفُوْا "And be not wasteful." Surah Al-An'am 6:141

Planning is Consistent with Tawakkul

Gathering resources and planning for the future is completely consistent with trust in Allah (Tawakkul). Allah explicitly commands Muslims to prepare strength:

وَاَعِدُّوْا لَهُمْ مَّا اسْتَطَعْتُمْ مِّنْ قُوَّةٍ "And prepare against them whatever force you can." Surah Al-Anfal 8:60

Examples of Planning in Seerah & History

  • Hazrat Yusuf (AS): Formulated a 15-year agricultural and grain-storage plan in Egypt. As a result, Egypt not only survived the famine but also exported grain to other nations.
  • Battle of the Trench (Ghazwah al-Khandaq): The Prophet ﷺ adopted Salman al-Farsi’s (RA) plan to dig a defensive trench around Madinah, successfully protecting the city.
  • Treaty of Hudaybiyyah: A masterpiece of long-term diplomatic strategy that laid the foundation for the peaceful expansion of Islam and the eventual Conquest of Makkah.

💪 Self-Reliance and Dignity of Labor (خود انحصاری)

Self-Reliance (خود انحصاری) means being independent, working hard, and relying on one’s own efforts rather than depending on external aid or charity.

وَاَنْ لَّیْسَ لِلْاِنْسَانِ اِلَّا مَا سَعٰی "And that there is not for man except that for which he strives." Surah An-Najm 53:39
اِنَّ اللّٰهَ لَا يُغَيِّرُ مَا بِقَوْمٍ حَتّٰى يُغَيِّرُوْا مَا بِاَنْفُسِهِمْ "Indeed, Allah will not change the condition of a people until they change what is in themselves." Surah Ar-Ra'd 13:11

Dignity of Manual Labor

Islam exalts physical labor and honest earning. The Prophet ﷺ said:

"Whosoever passes the evening exhausted due to hard manual labor passes that evening forgiven by Allah Almighty."
— Kanz-ul-Ummal

Earning a livelihood through manual work was the established practice of all Prophets (AS). True human dignity lies in working with one’s own hands.

🔬 Academic & Scientific Advancement (علمی ترقی)

A truly Islamic educational framework synthesizes spiritual/religious sciences with material/physical sciences. An educational system limited to only one of them is incomplete.

اَللّٰهُمَّ اِنِّیْ اَسْاَلُكَ عِلْمًا نَّافِعًا "O Allah, I ask You for beneficial knowledge..." Sunan Ibn Majah

Beneficial Knowledge (علمِ نافع) includes every discipline that serves humanity — Quranic sciences, Hadith, Fiqh, Medicine, Mathematics, Astronomy, Engineering, Economics, and Social Sciences.

Pioneering Muslim Scientists of the Golden Age

Ibn Sina (Avicenna)

Authored Al-Qanun fi al-Tibb (The Canon of Medicine), used as the official medical textbook in European universities until the 17th century.

Al-Khwarizmi

The father of Algebra. He separated Arithmetic from Algebra and introduced logical scientific approaches to mathematics.

Al-Zahrawi

Pioneered modern surgery and authored Al-Tasrif, detailing over 200 surgical instruments.

Al-Biruni

Master astronomer and geographer who accurately calculated the Earth’s circumference and documented planetary orbits.

Al-Jahiz

Authored Kitab al-Hayawan (Book of Animals), pioneering early zoological sciences.

Omar Khayyam

Developed the highly accurate Jalali solar calendar and contributed significantly to celestial observation.

To achieve a true renaissance, modern Muslims must master science and technology, foster inter-Islamic economic cooperation, and gain independence in global trade.

Q2. Short Questions

(i) What is meant by the Renaissance of Islam (نظامِ اسلام کی نشاۃِ ثانیہ)? ▼

It refers to the revival of Islamic civilizational leadership by uniting Muslims under Islamic principles, restoring intellectual and technological excellence, and serving both the rights of Allah and human welfare.

(ii) What is meant by self-reliance (خود انحصاری)? ▼

Self-reliance means being independent, striving diligently through personal effort, utilizing internal resources, and avoiding financial or material dependence on external forces or charity.

(iii) What guidance does the Seerah provide for reforming the educational system? ▼

The Seerah guides us to establish an integrated education system that combines spiritual/moral instruction with modern physical sciences. The Prophet ﷺ prayed for “beneficial knowledge” (علمِ نافع), which includes all disciplines that advance human welfare.

(iv) What lesson do Islamic teachings give regarding the dignity of labor? ▼

Islam accords immense dignity to physical labor. The Prophet ﷺ stated that one who spends the evening exhausted from manual labor passes that evening forgiven by Allah. Working with one’s hands is the Sunnah of all Prophets.

(v) Name any two famous Muslim scientists and their landmark books. ▼

1. Ibn Sina (Avicenna): Al-Qanun fi al-Tibb (The Canon of Medicine)
2. Abu al-Qasim al-Zahrawi: Al-Tasrif (on surgery and medical instruments)

Q3. Detailed Question

Elaborate in detail on the responsibilities of Muslims regarding the Renaissance of Islam. ▼

To achieve a true Islamic Renaissance, Muslims must fulfill these key responsibilities:

1. Strategic Planning: Implement long-term resource management and defense capability as directed in Surah Al-Anfal (8:60) and demonstrated in Ghazwah al-Khandaq.

2. Self-Reliance & Hard Work: Cultivate internal economic independence based on Surah An-Najm (53:39), honoring manual work and eliminating reliance on foreign aid.

3. Integrated Educational Reform: Combine sacred religious sciences with modern STEM fields to produce well-rounded, ethical leaders.

4. Reviving Scientific Inquiry: Reclaim the legacy of Golden Age scholars (Ibn Sina, Al-Khwarizmi, Al-Biruni) by investing in research and technological innovation.

5. Global Unity & Economic Cooperation: Foster economic alliances among Muslim nations to achieve independence in global trade.
