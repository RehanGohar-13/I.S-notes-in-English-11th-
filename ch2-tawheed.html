Tawheed — Oneness of Allah

Chapter 2(A) — Faith (Imaniyat) · Textbook pp. 13–19

بِسْمِ اللهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيْمِ "In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful."

🎯 Learning Outcomes

After studying this lesson, students will be able to:

  1. Understand the meaning and concept of Tawheed.
  2. Be aware of the evidences for the existence of Allah and the proofs of Tawheed.
  3. Gain familiarity with the kinds of Tawheed and types of Shirk, and the requirements of Tawheed.
  4. Know the proofs of Tawheed and remain free from Shirk (polytheism).
  5. Analyze the benefits and fruits of Tawheed and the harms of Shirk in practical life.
  6. Strengthen their faith (Iman) by understanding the importance of Tawheed.
  7. Be aware of the proofs of Allah's existence and protect themselves from disbelief (Kufr) and atheism (Ilhad).

📖 What is Tawheed?

Linguistic Meaning

The word Tawheed (توحید) literally means "to consider one" and "to know as unique, singular, and unmatched."

Technical / Religious Meaning

In Islamic terminology, Tawheed means to believe in Allah as alone, singular, and without partner in:

1. Zaat (ذات)

His Essence / Divine Being

2. Sifaat (صفات)

His Attributes & Qualities

3. Ruboobiyyat (ربوبیت)

His Lordship (Sole Creator, Owner & Sustainer)

4. Uloohiyyat (الوہیت)

His Divinity & Right to be Worshipped

In short: believing that Allah is One and without any partner in His Essence, Attributes, Lordship, Divinity, and Worship — this is Tawheed.

Every single Prophet (AS) — from Adam (AS) to Muhammad ﷺ — called mankind to the same primary message: the Oneness of Allah. Tawheed is the original, innate, and eternal religion of all humanity.

🌱 Tawheed is the Natural Disposition (Fitrah)

Allah created human beings upon the natural instinct (Fitrah) of Tawheed. If left to their pure nature, humans will instinctively choose monotheism — because the concept of Allah's Oneness is embedded in the very fabric of human conscience.

فَاَقِمْ وَجْهَكَ لِلدِّيْنِ حَنِيْفًا ؕ فِطْرَتَ اللهِ الَّتِيْ فَطَرَ النَّاسَ عَلَيْهَا "So direct your face toward the religion with true devotion — (this is) the nature (Fitrah) of Allah upon which He has created all people." Surah Ar-Rum 30:30

Every child is born upon this Fitrah; external influences and upbringing may distort it later.

🔍 Evidences for the Existence and Oneness of Allah

1. The Argument from Design (Dalil-e-Nazm)

Organized design necessitates a conscious designer:

⌚

The Watch

Requires a watchmaker

🏗️

The Building

Requires an architect

🌌

The Universe

Requires a Supreme Creator

اَمْ خُلِقُوْا مِنْ غَيْرِ شَيْءٍ اَمْ هُمُ الْخٰلِقُوْنَؕ اَمْ خَلَقُوا السَّمٰوٰتِ وَ الْاَرْضَؕ بَلْ لَّا يُوْقِنُوْنَؕ "Were they created by nothing, or were they themselves the creators? Or did they create the heavens and the earth? Rather, they are not certain." Surah At-Toor 52:35-36

2. The Perfect Order and Cosmic Harmony

The universe functions with flawless mathematical precision. Allah challenges us to find a single flaw:

الَّذِي خَلَقَ سَبْعَ سَمَاوَاتٍ طِبَاقًا ۖ مَّا تَرَىٰ فِي خَلْقِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ مِن تَفَاوُتٍ ۖ فَارْجِعِ الْبَصَرَ هَلْ تَرَىٰ مِن فُطُورٍ ثُمَّ ارْجِعِ الْبَصَرَ كَرَّتَيْنِ يَنقَلِبْ إِلَيْكَ الْبَصَرُ خَاسِئًا وَهُوَ حَسِيرٌ "He Who created seven heavens in layers. You see no inconsistency in the creation of the Most Merciful. Look again — do you see any flaws? Look again and again — your sight will return humbled and exhausted." Surah Al-Mulk 67:3-4
لَا الشَّمْسُ يَنْۢبَغِيْ لَهَاۤ اَنْ تُدْرِکَ الْقَمَرَ وَ لَا الَّيْلُ سَابِقُ النَّہَارِؕ وَ كُلٌّ فِيْ فَلَکٍ يَّسْبَحُوْنَ "The sun cannot overtake the moon, nor does the night outstrip the day; each swims in its own orbit." Surah Ya-Seen 36:40

3. The Argument from Coherence (Unity of Command)

If multiple gods existed, conflicting wills would destroy the universe:

لَوْ كَانَ فِيْهِمَاۤ اٰلِهَةٌ اِلَّا اللهُ لَفَسَدَتَا "Had there been gods besides Allah in the heavens and earth, both would surely have been ruined." Surah Al-Anbiya 21:22

4. The Proof within the Human Self (Anfus)

وَ فِيْۤ اَنْفُسِکُمْؕ اَفَلَا تُبْصِرُوْنَ "And within your own selves (are signs). Do you not then see?" Surah Adh-Dhariyat 51:21

The human neurological, circulatory, and sensory systems are more advanced than any human-engineered technology. Deep reflection on our biology confirms a Singular, Supreme Creator.

🏷️ The 4 Kinds of Tawheed

1. Tawheed fi-dh-Dhaat

Oneness in Essence

Allah is absolutely unique, indivisible, and singular in His Being. He neither begets nor is begotten. He has no parts, partners, or equals.

2. Tawheed-ur-Ruboobiyyah

Oneness in Lordship

Allah is the sole Creator, Owner, Sustainer, and Ruler. He alone gives life, death, and provision. No one shares His control.

3. Tawheed-ul-Uloohiyyah

Oneness in Worship

Allah alone is the true Deity worthy of worship. All prayers, sacrifices, vows, and obedience belong exclusively to Him.

4. Tawheed-al-Asma was-Sifaat

Oneness in Names & Attributes

Allah is peerless in all His Names and Attributes. No creation shares His divine qualities in their absolute, perfect sense.

⚠️ Types of Shirk (شرک کی اقسام)

Shirk literally means "partnership" and "association." In Shariah, it means setting up partners with Allah in His Essence, Attributes, or the Requirements of His Attributes.

1. Shirk in Essence (ذات میں شرک)

Believing another entity shares Allah's divine reality — e.g., believing in multiple gods, or that Allah has children/parents.

لَمْ يَلِدْ ۙ وَ لَمْ يُوْلَدْ "He neither begets nor is He begotten." Surah Al-Ikhlas 112:3

2. Shirk in Attributes (صفات میں شرک)

Attributing divine qualities to creation — e.g., believing a human has independent all-encompassing knowledge, absolute power, or is eternal by nature.

لَیْسَ کَمِثْلِهٖ شَیْءٌ "There is nothing like unto Him." Surah Ash-Shura 42:11

3. Shirk in Requirements of Attributes (صفات کے تقاضوں میں شرک)

Directing worship, prostration, absolute obedience, or ultimate reliance to anyone other than Allah — e.g., bowing before idols, saints, or following man-made laws that contradict Shariah.

اَلَّا تَعْبُدُوْۤا اِلَّاۤ اِیَّاہُ "That you worship none but Him alone." Surah Al-Isra 17:23
· · ·

⚖️ Requirements of Faith in Tawheed

Shirk is not limited to worshipping idols of wood or stone. It also includes placing ultimate hope, fear, and desperation in created beings rather than Allah. The core demands of Tawheed are:

  • ❤️ Love of Allah (Mahabbat-e-Ilahi)
  • 📜 Obedience to Allah (Ita'at-e-Ilahi)
  • 🕌 Obedience to the Prophet ﷺ
  • 🛡️ Fear & Awe of Allah (Khashyat-e-Ilahi)
  • ⚓ Reliance on Allah (Tawakkul)
  • 🤲 Supplication (Du'a to Allah alone)
  • 🕯️ Vows (Nadhr for Allah alone)
  • 🙇 Prostration (Sajdah to Allah alone)
· · ·

🌟 Fruits & Benefits of Tawheed in Practical Life

1. Protection of Self-Respect

The believer attaches his heart only to Allah, asks only from Him, and fears only Him — liberating himself from bowing before worldly tyrants and false deities.

2. Wealth of Humility

Knowing that honor and dishonor are in Allah's hands alone, the monotheist walks with genuine modesty instead of arrogance and ego.

3. Determination & Courage

All hopes anchored in Allah. Unshakable faith that every righteous effort will be rewarded in the Hereafter, empowering endurance and perseverance.

4. Steadfastness & Bravery

Life, death, benefit, and harm are in Allah's hands alone. Fear of worldly creation vanishes, enabling courage for truth and justice.

5. Contentment & Self-Sufficiency (Qana'at & Istighna)

All treasures of sustenance belong to Allah. The believer becomes independent of people — never desperate, never greedy, and never humiliated before others for worldly gain.

6. Balanced Personality Development

Life's purpose becomes clear. Every action is for Allah's pleasure. This produces patience (Sabr), gratitude (Shukr), reliance (Tawakkul), composure, balance, and inner peace (Itminan).

· · ·

🚫 Harms of Shirk in Practical Life

Deprivation from Tawheed and the commission of Shirk produce devastating effects:

1. Degradation of Human Dignity

When a person bows before stones, trees, idols, and created objects, he insults his own humanity — degrading himself below the status Allah honored him with.

وَمَنْ يُّشْرِكْ بِاللّٰهِ فَكَأَنَّمَا خَرَّ مِنَ السَّمَآءِ فَتَخْطَفُهُ الطَّيْرُ اَوْ تَهْوِيْ بِهِ الرِّيْحُ "Whoever associates partners with Allah — it is as though he has fallen from the sky, and the birds snatch him away, or the wind carries him off to a distant place." Surah Al-Hajj 22:31
The Metaphor: The Mushrik is like someone plummeting from the sky — helpless. Birds scatter his pieces (just as his devotion is scattered among false gods) or wind blows him to a wasteland (just as Shirk blows him far from truth). Total destruction and disorientation.

2. Fear, Anxiety & Mental Disturbance

The Mushrik lives in constant fear and superstition, worried about the displeasure of countless imaginary deities and forces.

3. Loss of Focus and Purpose

Shirk strips a person of single-mindedness. Instead of one clear direction, life fragments into confusion — chasing multiple false sources of power.

Summary: Tawheed → Self-respect, humility, courage, contentment, balance, peace. Shirk → Degradation, fear, anxiety, confusion, loss of purpose.

Q2. Short Questions

(i) Write the meaning and concept of Tawheed. ▼

Linguistically, Tawheed means "to consider one." In Islamic terminology, it means believing in Allah as alone and without partner in His Essence (Zaat), Attributes (Sifaat), Lordship (Ruboobiyyat), Divinity (Uloohiyyat), and Worship. Every Prophet from Adam (AS) to Muhammad ﷺ called people to this same message.

(ii) Write one proof of Allah's existence from the phenomena of nature. ▼

The Argument from Design: Just as a watch requires a watchmaker and a building requires an architect, this vast, intricately ordered universe requires a Supreme Creator. It is logically impossible for the cosmos to have come into existence on its own. Allah says: "Were they created by nothing, or were they themselves the creators?" (52:35)

(iii) Write one proof of the Oneness (Wahdaniyat) of Allah. ▼

The Argument from Coherence: The universe operates with absolute coordination and zero contradiction. If there were multiple gods with conflicting wills, the cosmos would collapse into chaos. Allah says: "Had there been gods besides Allah in the heavens and earth, both would have been ruined." (21:22)

(iv) Describe any two kinds of Tawheed. ▼

1. Tawheed fi-dh-Dhaat (Oneness in Essence): Allah is absolutely unique and singular in His Being. He neither begets nor is begotten, and has no equal.

2. Tawheed-ul-Uloohiyyah (Oneness in Worship): Allah alone is the true Deity worthy of worship. All prayers, sacrifices, and obedience belong exclusively to Him.

(v) Review the harms of Shirk in practical life. ▼

Shirk produces devastating effects: (1) Degradation of human dignity — bowing before created objects insults humanity (22:31). (2) Fear, anxiety, and mental disturbance — the Mushrik lives in constant superstition. (3) Loss of focus and purpose — devotion is scattered among false gods, stripping the person of single-mindedness and coherent action.

Q3. Detailed Question

Write about the importance, benefits, and fruits of Tawheed. ▼

Tawheed is the foundation of Islam and the message of every Prophet. Its importance and fruits include:

1. Self-Respect: The believer bows only to Allah, freeing himself from worldly masters.

2. Humility: Knowing honor belongs to Allah alone removes arrogance.

3. Courage & Determination: Faith in Allah's justice and the Hereafter empowers perseverance.

4. Steadfastness: Knowing life and death are in Allah's hands eliminates fear of creation.

5. Contentment: All sustenance comes from Allah, making the believer independent of people.

6. Balanced Personality: A clear life purpose produces patience, gratitude, reliance, and inner peace.

In contrast, Shirk degrades dignity, breeds fear and anxiety, and fragments purpose. Tawheed is the path to success in both worlds.
