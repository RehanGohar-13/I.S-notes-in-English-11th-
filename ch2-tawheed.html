After studying this lesson, students will be able to:

  2. Be aware of the evidences for the existence of Allah and the proofs of Tawheed.

📖 What is Tawheed?

Linguistic Meaning

The word Tawheed (توحید) literally means "to consider one" and "to know as unique, singular, and unmatched."

Technical / Religious Meaning

In Islamic terminology, Tawheed means to believe in Allah as alone, singular, and without partner in:

1. Zaat (ذات)

His Essence / Divine Being

2. Sifaat (صفات)

His Attributes & Qualities

3. Ruboobiyyat (ربوبیت)

His Lordship (Sole Creator, Owner & Sustainer)

4. Uloohiyyat (الوہیت)

His Divinity & Right to be Worshipped

In short: believing that Allah is One and without any partner in His Essence, Attributes, Lordship, Divinity, and Worship — this is Tawheed.

Every single Prophet (AS) sent by Allah — from Adam (AS) to Muhammad ﷺ — called mankind to the exact same primary message: the Oneness of Allah and worshipping Him alone. Tawheed is not a newly developed concept; it is the original, innate, and eternal religion of all humanity.

🌱 Tawheed is the Natural Disposition (Fitrah)

Allah created human beings upon the natural instinct (Fitrah) of Tawheed. If a person is left to their pure, uncorrupted nature, they will instinctively choose the religion of monotheism — because the concept of Allah's existence and His Oneness is embedded in the very fabric of human conscience.

Allah says:

فَاَقِمْ وَجْهَكَ لِلدِّيْنِ حَنِيْفًا ؕ فِطْرَتَ اللهِ الَّتِيْ فَطَرَ النَّاسَ عَلَيْهَا "So direct your face toward the religion with true devotion — (this is) the nature (Fitrah) of Allah upon which He has created all people." Surah Ar-Rum 30:30

This verse proves that Tawheed is not an artificial construct — it is the default state of the human soul. Every individual is born upon this Fitrah; external societal influences and deviations corrupt it later.

🔍 Evidences for the Existence and Oneness of Allah

The textbook details four logical, observable, and existential arguments demonstrating the existence and absolute Oneness of Allah:

1. The Argument from Design (Dalil-e-Nazm)

Human intellect confirms that organized and purposeful design necessitates a conscious designer:

⌚

The Watch

Requires a watchmaker

🏗️

The Building

Requires an architect

🌌

The Universe

Requires a Supreme Creator

It is logically impossible for this massive cosmos with its countless intricate systems to have generated itself out of pure nothingness. Allah challenges this irrationality in the Quran:

اَمْ خُلِقُوْا مِنْ غَيْرِ شَيْءٍ اَمْ هُمُ الْخٰلِقُوْنَؕ اَمْ خَلَقُوا السَّمٰوٰتِ وَ الْاَرْضَؕ بَلْ لَّا يُوْقِنُوْنَؕ "Were they created by nothing, or were they themselves the creators? Or did they create the heavens and the earth? Rather, they are not certain." Surah At-Toor 52:35-36

2. The Perfect Order and Cosmic Harmony

The universe functions with flawless mathematical accuracy and consistency. Planets follow precise orbits, day and night rotate systematically, and ecological systems operate in perfect equilibrium. Allah challenges mankind to spot even a single flaw in His design:

الَّذِي خَلَقَ سَبْعَ سَمَاوَاتٍ طِبَاقًا ۖ مَّا تَرَىٰ فِي خَلْقِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ مِن تَفَاوُتٍ ۖ فَارْجِعِ الْبَصَرَ هَلْ تَرَىٰ مِن فُطُورٍ ثُمَّ ارْجِعِ الْبَصَرَ كَرَّتَيْنِ يَنقَلِبْ إِلَيْكَ الْبَصَرُ خَاسِئًا وَهُوَ حَسِيرٌ "He Who created seven heavens in layers. You do not see in the creation of the Most Merciful any inconsistency. So look again: do you see any flaws? Then look again and again — your sight will return to you humbled and exhausted." Surah Al-Mulk 67:3-4

The celestial bodies operate on a strict, collision-free schedule:

لَا الشَّمْسُ يَنْۢبَغِيْ لَهَاۤ اَنْ تُدْرِکَ الْقَمَرَ وَ لَا الَّيْلُ سَابِقُ النَّہَارِؕ وَ كُلٌّ فِيْ فَلَکٍ يَّسْبَحُوْنَ "It is not allowable for the sun to reach the moon, nor does the night outstrip the day; but each swims along in its own orbit." Surah Ya-Seen 36:40

3. The Argument from Coherence (Unity of Command)

The complete absence of conflicting laws across the cosmos is undeniable evidence that **the Ruler is One**. If multiple sovereign deities existed, conflicting wills would inevitably clash, resulting in catastrophic breakdown and cosmic ruin:

لَوْ كَانَ فِيْهِمَاۤ اٰلِهَةٌ اِلَّا اللهُ لَفَسَدَتَا "Had there been within the heavens and earth gods besides Allah, both would surely have been ruined." Surah Al-Anbiya 21:22

4. The Proof within the Human Self (Anfus)

Allah has embedded signs of His existence and brilliance directly within human anatomy, physiology, and consciousness:

وَ فِيْۤ اَنْفُسِکُمْؕ اَفَلَا تُبْصِرُوْنَ "And within your own selves (are signs). Do you not then see?" Surah Adh-Dhariyat 51:21
  • The human neurological, circulatory, and sensory networks are more advanced than any human-engineered supercomputer.
  • Reflecting on our biological design leaves no doubt that human life is the deliberate masterpiece of a Singular, Supreme Creator.

🏷️ The 4 Kinds of Tawheed

1. Tawheed fi-dh-Dhaat (توحید فی الذات)

Oneness in Essence

Believing that Allah is absolutely unique, indivisible, and singular in His Divine Being.

  • He has no parts, partners, or equal counterparts.
  • He is self-sufficient (*As-Samad*).
  • He neither begets nor is He begotten (has no parents, children, or lineage).

2. Tawheed-ur-Ruboobiyyah (توحید ربوبیت)

Oneness in Lordship

Believing that Allah is the sole Creator, Absolute Sovereign, Sustainer (Razzaq), and Administrator of everything.

  • He alone brings into existence, provides food, grants life, and causes death.

Allah says:

"Allah is the One Who created you, then provided for you, then causes you to die, and then will bring you back to life. Is there any of your 'partners' who can do anything of that? Exalted is He far above what they associate with Him." Surah Ar-Rum 30:40

3. Tawheed-ul-Uloohiyyah (توحید الوہیت)

Oneness in Worship

Believing that Allah alone is the true Deity worthy of worship, devotion, and unconditional submission.

  • Worshipping any created entity is strictly forbidden.
  • All prayers, sacrifices, vows, and life affairs must align exclusively with His commands.

Allah says:

"So whoever hopes for the meeting with his Lord — let him do righteous deeds and not associate anyone in the worship of his Lord." Surah Al-Kahf 18:110

4. Tawheed-al-Asma was-Sifaat (توحید اسما و صفات)

Oneness in Names & Attributes

Believing that Allah is peerless and supreme in all His Divine Names and Attributes.

  • Just as His Essence is unique, His perfect attributes (e.g., Infinite Knowledge, Absolute Power, Absolute Hearing) have no equal.
  • No created entity possesses attributes in the absolute, independent, and divine manner that Allah does.

⚠️ Types of Shirk (شرک کی اقسام)

Linguistic Meaning: The word Shirk (شرک) literally signifies "partnership" (Hissadari) and "sharing / association" (Sajhepan).

Technical / Religious Meaning: In Shariah terminology, Shirk means to set up partners, equals, or associates with Allah in His Essence (Zaat), Attributes (Sifaat), or the Practical Requirements of His Attributes (Taqada-e-Sifaat).

Shirk is classified into three main categories:

1. Shirk in Essence (ذات میں شرک — Shirk fi-dh-Dhaat)

This means believing that another entity shares in the fundamental divine reality, essence, or nature of Allah.

Common Forms:

  • Believing in dualism (two gods) or trinity (three gods), or considering any entity equal to Allah.
  • Believing that Allah has offspring (a son or daughter), or that Allah Himself was born of parents — because a parent and child share the same essential nature.

The Holy Quran decisively refutes this form of Shirk:

لَمْ يَلِدْ ۙ وَ لَمْ يُوْلَدْ "He neither begets nor is He begotten." Surah Al-Ikhlas 112:3

2. Shirk in Attributes (صفات میں شرک — Shirk fi-s-Sifaat)

This means attributing divine, infinite, and inherent qualities identical to Allah's attributes to any created being.

Common Forms:

  • Believing a human, angel, or saint possesses all-encompassing, independent knowledge of the unseen (*Ilm-e-Ghayb*) like Allah.
  • Believing another entity has independent absolute power (*Qadir-e-Mutlaq*) or sovereign divine will.
  • Believing any creation is eternal without beginning (*Azali*) or eternal without end (*Abadi*) by its own intrinsic nature.

The Quran establishes the utter incomparability of Allah's attributes:

لَیْسَ کَمِثْلِهٖ شَیْءٌ "There is nothing like unto Him." Surah Ash-Shura 42:11

3. Shirk in the Requirements of Attributes (صفات کے تقاضوں میں شرک)

Because Allah alone possesses absolute divine attributes, the sole logical demand (Taqada) is that all acts of worship, ultimate devotion, physical bowing, and absolute obedience belong exclusively to Him.

Common Forms:

  • Prostrating (*Sajdah*) or performing acts of worship before anyone other than Allah.
  • Treating any created being as the ultimate solver of affairs (*Haqeeqi Karsaz*) or holder of absolute sovereignty (*Iqtidaar-e-Aala*).
  • Enforcing or following man-made commands that directly contradict Allah's explicit divine laws.

Allah explicitly commands:

اَلَّا تَعْبُدُوْۤا اِلَّاۤ اِیَّاہُ "That you worship none but Him alone." Surah Al-Isra 17:23
A true believer must acknowledge Allah as the Sole Giver of all blessings (*Mun'im-e-Haqeeqi*), express heartfelt gratitude (*Shukr*), and turn solely to His infinite grace as the ultimate source of help during hardship.

⚖️ Practical Requirements of Faith in Tawheed (عقیدہ توحید کے تقاضے)

A true understanding of Tawheed demands that a Muslim recognize that Shirk is not limited to worshipping idols of wood or stone. Shirk also occurs when a person places ultimate reliance, hope, or reverential fear in created beings rather than Allah.

Countless individuals profess faith in Allah with their tongues, but practically plead before mortal humans with a degree of desperation, humility, and submission that belongs exclusively to Allah for their livelihood, health, problems, and progeny.

The Core Demands of Tawheed:

  • ❤️ Love of Allah (محبتِ الٰہی): Loving Allah above all else.
  • 📜 Obedience to Allah (اطاعتِ الٰہی): Submitting to His Shariah.
  • 🕌 Obedience to the Prophet ﷺ (اطاعتِ رسول ﷺ): Following the Sunnah as the practical model.
  • 🛡️ Fear & Awe of Allah (خشیتِ الٰہی): Fearing His displeasure alone.
  • ⚓ Reliance on Allah (توکل): Trusting completely in Allah's decree.
  • 🤲 Supplication (دعا): Directing prayers and needs solely to Him.
  • 🕯️ Vows & Oaths (نذر): Dedicating religious vows exclusively to Allah.
  • 🙇 Prostration (سجدہ): Bowing heads and placing foreheads on the ground only for Allah.

🌟 Fruits and Benefits of Tawheed in Practical Life (عملی زندگی میں توحید کے فوائد و ثمرات)

Internalizing the reality of Tawheed produces profound, transformative psychological and behavioral improvements in a person's life:

1. Protection of Self-Respect (عزتِ نفس کی حفاظت)

Tawheed grants human beings the highest degree of self-respect and dignity. When a person firmly believes that the sole Creator, Owner, Sustainer, and Ruler of the universe is Allah alone, he attaches his heart only to Him.

He asks only from Allah and harbors fear only for Allah — permanently liberating himself from demeaning himself or bowing before worldly tyrants, wealthy elites, and false deities.

2. The Wealth of Humility (عجز وانکساری کی دولت)

Tawheed eradicates arrogance, vanity, and egotism (*Kibr*). The believer understands that absolute supremacy belongs only to Allah. It is Allah who grants authority, status, and wealth, and He holds the power to withdraw them at any second.

Because honor and dishonor (*Izzat wa Dhillat*) are exclusively in Allah's hands, the true monotheist walks with genuine modesty, humility, and compassion.

3. High Determination & Courage (عزم و ہمت)

A true monotheist is a paragon of resolve and fortitude. All his hopes and aspirations are anchored in Allah's infinite power and justice.

He maintains unshakable faith that every sincere righteous effort will be fully rewarded in the Hereafter (*Akhirah*), empowering him to endure difficulties with optimism, courage, and perseverance.

4. Steadfastness & Bravery (استقامت اور بہادری)

Belief in Tawheed instills legendary bravery and moral consistency. The believer knows with absolute conviction that life, death, benefit, and harm are governed exclusively by Allah.

Since no mortal power can cause him death or harm before his decreed appointed time (*Ajal*), fear of worldly creation vanishes, enabling him to stand courageously for truth and justice.

📋 Quick Summary: Kinds of Tawheed vs. Types of Shirk

Category Tawheed (Affirmation of Oneness) Shirk (Polytheistic Association)
Essence (Zaat) Allah is One, Indivisible, neither begets nor is begotten (112:3). Believing in dualism/trinity or attributing sons/daughters to Allah.
Attributes (Sifaat) Allah's qualities are unmatched; nothing is like Him (42:11). Believing creation has independent omniscience (*Ilm-e-Ghayb*) or omnipotence.
Worship & Demands (Taqada) Worship, prostration, and absolute obedience belong to Allah alone (17:23). Bowing/prostrating to mortals or following laws contrary to Shariah.
Lordship (Ruboobiyyat) Allah alone creates, provides sustenance, gives life and death (30:40). Believing saints/spirits control worldly sustenance and destiny.
