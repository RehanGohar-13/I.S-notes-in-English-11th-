Salah — The Philosophy of Prayer

Chapter 2(A) — Ibadaat (Acts of Worship) · Philosophy of Namaz · Textbook pp. 38–42

بِسْمِ اللهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيْمِ "In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful."

🎯 Learning Outcomes

After studying this lesson, students will be able to:

  1. Understand the importance, philosophy, and rulings of Salah.
  2. Evaluate the spiritual, physical, and social effects of Salah.
  3. Achieve purification of the self (tazkiyah-e-nafs) through khushu, khuzu, and ikhlas.
  4. Recognize the importance of khushu, khuzu, and ikhlas in prayer.
  5. Observe congregational prayer (Namaz-e-Ba-Jama'ah) regularly in practical life.

⭐ The Importance of Salah

Salah is the most important pillar of Islam after the Shahadah of Tawheed and Risalat. It holds a unique place among the commandments of Islam, and the Quran repeatedly commands its establishment:

وَ اَقِیْمُوا الصَّلٰوةَ وَ لَا تَكُوْنُوْا مِنَ الْمُشْرِكِیْنَ "And establish prayer and do not be among the polytheists." Surah Ar-Rum 30:31

The Prophet ﷺ said about the foundational pillars of Islam:

بُنِیَ الْاِسْلَامُ عَلٰى خَمْسٍ "Islam is built upon five: the testimony that there is no god but Allah and that Muhammad ﷺ is the Messenger of Allah; establishing Salah; giving Zakat; performing Hajj; and fasting Ramadan." Sahih Bukhari: 8

The Prophet ﷺ also declared Salah as the pillar of the entire religion:

"The head of the matter is Islam, and its pillar (Amood) is Salah." Jami' Tirmidhi: 2616

Universal Obligation: Salah was obligatory on all previous nations as well. Every Prophet (AS) emphasized Salah to his people. The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ also stressed Salah upon his Ummah, saying that the first question on the Day of Qiyamah will be about Salah. (Sunan Ibn Majah: 1425)

The obligation (fardhiyyah) of Salah is proven by both the Quran and Sunnah. Whoever denies, doubts, or mocks the obligation of Salah leaves the fold of Islam.

The Prophet ﷺ gave a beautiful analogy to encourage the Companions:

"What do you think if there is a river in front of your house in which you bathe five times every day — would any dirt remain on his body?" The Companions replied: "No dirt would remain." He ﷺ said: "This is the example of the five prayers — Allah wipes away sins through them." Sahih Muslim: 667

The Prophet ﷺ also instructed: "Pray as you have seen me pray." (Sahih Bukhari: 6008)

🕌 The Philosophy of Salah (فلسفۂ نماز)

The fundamental purpose of Salah is to establish a strong spiritual connection between Allah ﷻ and His servant. Salah is the most effective means of attaining closeness (qurb) to Allah. The Prophet ﷺ said:

"When one of you is in prayer, he is in private conversation (whispering) with his Lord." Sahih Bukhari: 417

What Praying Five Times a Day Produces

  • 💗 A deep spiritual bond with Allah ﷻ — producing love, fear (khawf), and awe (khashyat) in the heart.
  • 🙇 An opportunity to bow before Allah, express humility, and acknowledge His greatness.
  • 🤲 A constant reminder that man is in need of Allah, and his entire life depends on His grace.
  • 🕋 Sajdah — a key part of prayer — develops humility and submission, and protects one from the arrogance and pride of the self (nafs).
  • 🛡️ Salah shields the believer from sins and evils.
اِنَّ الصَّلٰوةَ تَنْهٰى عَنِ الْفَحْشَآءِ وَالْمُنْكَرِ "Indeed, prayer prevents indecency and evil." Surah Al-Ankabut 29:45
Salah cleanses the heart, purifies thoughts and deeds, and inspires a righteous life. By praying five times a day, one is never heedless of Allah's remembrance. Repeatedly standing before Allah with wudu, bowing, and prostrating guarantees obedience, submission, and love — saving a person from heedlessness (ghaflah) and sin.

📋 Rulings of Salah (نماز کے احکامات)

Salah is obligatory in every condition — travel, residence, war, peace, prosperity, grief, health, and sickness. It is never excused. However, for the convenience of the Ummah, the Prophet ﷺ granted the following concessions:

  • Shortening (qasr) in certain unavoidable situations
  • Praying sitting, lying down, or by gestures when ill
  • Tayammum when water is unavailable or in severe sickness

1 Fajr Pre-dawn

3 Asr Late afternoon
4 Maghrib After sunset

Conditions (Shara'it) of Salah

Wudu is a prerequisite — no prayer is valid without it. Other conditions include:

  • Purity of the place of prayer
  • Purity of body and clothes
  • Covering of the satr (private parts)
  • Prayer time having entered
  • Facing the Qibla
  • Making the niyyah (intention)

Disliked Acts During Prayer

The following acts damage the khushu & khuzu of prayer and should be avoided:

  • ❌ Moving the eyes around / looking up at the sky
  • ❌ Continuously adjusting clothes
  • ❌ Cracking fingers
  • ❌ Clearing the throat unnecessarily
  • ❌ Fidgeting / excessive movement

💗 Khushu, Khuzu & Ikhlas — The Soul of Prayer

Khushu (خشوع)

The outer limbs bowing before Allah ﷻ with humility and submission.

Khuzu (خضوع)

The heart and thoughts turning fully towards Allah ﷻ.

Khushu & Khuzu together are called the SOUL of Salah.
قَدْ اَفْلَحَ الْمُؤْمِنُوْنَ ۝ الَّذِیْنَ هُمْ فِیْ صَلَاتِهِمْ خٰشِعُوْنَ "Certainly, the believers have succeeded — those who are humble (khashi'un) in their prayers." Surah Al-Mu'minoon 23:1-2

The Prophet's ﷺ own khushu is described in Hadith:

A sound used to come from the Prophet's ﷺ chest due to weeping, like the sound of a grinding stone. Sunan Abi Dawood: 904

Hazrat Uthman Ghani (RA) narrated:

I heard the Messenger of Allah ﷺ say: "Any Muslim who, when the time of a fardh prayer comes, performs wudu well and prays with khushu and khuzu — that prayer becomes an atonement (kaffarah) for all his previous sins." Sahih Muslim: 228

Ikhlas — Sincerity in Prayer

Alongside khushu & khuzu, ikhlas (sincerity) is emphasized, and showing off (riya) is strictly forbidden.

فَاعْبُدِ اللهَ مُخْلِصًا لَّهُ الدِّیْنَ "So worship Allah, making the religion sincerely for Him." Surah Az-Zumar 39:2
The Prophet ﷺ warned: "Whoever prays to show off has committed shirk." (Musnad Ahmad: 17140)

The philosophy of establishing Salah is precisely this: that prayer be offered with khushu, khuzu, and ikhlas — observing all conditions and etiquette, and avoiding anything that destroys its reward.

👥 Namaz-e-Ba-Jama'ah (Congregational Prayer)

Along with establishing Salah, both the Quran and Hadith emphasize praying in congregation:

وَ اَقِیْمُوا الصَّلٰوةَ وَ اٰتُوا الزَّكٰوةَ وَ ارْكَعُوْا مَعَ الرّٰكِعِیْنَ "And establish prayer, give Zakat, and bow with those who bow." Surah Al-Baqarah 2:43

Rewards of Congregational Prayer

🌟

25×

Reward equal to 25 individual prayers
Sahih Muslim: 649

🌟

27×

Reward equal to 27 individual prayers
Sahih Bukhari: 645

Why Five Daily Adhans? The call to prayer five times a day draws attention to the fact that Salah should be offered in congregation. Praying in jama'ah establishes an atmosphere of discipline, trains people in obedience to laws, announces the revival of Islam's social system, and brings to life the concept of Islamic civilizational beauty.

🌸 Benefits of Salah

Offering Salah with full etiquette brings countless spiritual, physical, and social benefits:

🕊️ Spiritual Benefits

  • Obedience to Allah's command
  • Permanent connection with Allah ﷻ
  • Peace & tranquility through dhikr
  • Preservation of the sense of servitude (bandagi)
  • Allah's pleasure and immense reward
  • Forgiveness of sins
  • Protection from evils

💪 Physical Benefits

  • Washing hands, face, and feet five times a day in wudu keeps the body clean, pure, and fresh
  • Positive effects on overall health
  • Ruku and Sujud have numerous medical benefits
  • In sujud, blood flows easily and abundantly to the vessels of the head → the brain is refreshed

Social Benefits of Praying in Jama'ah

The strong encouragement to pray in congregation is specifically so that these social and collective benefits are achieved:

  • 👑 Spirit of obedience to the Ameer (leader)
  • ❤️ Mutual sympathy, love, and unity (yagangi)
  • 🤝 Awareness of other Muslim brothers' circumstances → nurturing sentiments of compassion (gham-gusari)
  • ⚖️ Discouragement of social disparity
  • 🧎 Standing in one row teaches the lesson of equality (musawat) and brotherhood
Conclusion: We should establish Salah with khushu & khuzu in the light of Quran and Sunnah, so that we may please Allah ﷻ and His Messenger ﷺ, and succeed in both this world and the Hereafter.

Q2. Short Questions

(i) Briefly describe the importance of Salah. ▼

Salah is the most important pillar of Islam after the Shahadah. The Quran repeatedly commands its establishment (Surah Ar-Rum 30:31). The Prophet ﷺ called it the pillar (Amood) of the Deen (Tirmidhi 2616), and it is the first thing a person will be questioned about on the Day of Qiyamah (Ibn Majah 1425). It was obligatory on every previous nation. Denying, doubting, or mocking Salah takes one out of Islam. The five daily prayers wash away sins just as bathing in a river five times washes away dirt (Muslim 667).

(ii) What effects does congregational prayer have on practical life? ▼

Praying in jama'ah has powerful social effects: it builds the spirit of obedience to the Ameer, cultivates mutual love, sympathy, and unity, creates awareness of fellow Muslims' circumstances (fostering compassion), discourages social disparity, and — by standing shoulder to shoulder in one row — teaches the lesson of equality and brotherhood. It establishes an atmosphere of discipline and trains people in obedience to law. Its reward is 27 times that of individual prayer (Bukhari 645).

(iii) What is meant by Khushu and Khuzu in Salah? ▼

Khushu means the bowing of the outer limbs before Allah ﷻ with humility and submission. Khuzu means the heart and thoughts turning fully towards Allah ﷻ. Together, khushu and khuzu are called the soul of Salah. Allah ﷻ praises such believers: "Certainly, the believers have succeeded — those who are humble in their prayers" (Surah Al-Mu'minoon 23:1-2). A prayer offered with khushu and khuzu becomes an atonement for all previous sins (Muslim 228).

(iv) Write any two conditions of Salah. ▼

1. Wudu (ablution): No prayer is valid without wudu. In severe illness or absence of water, tayammum is permitted.

2. Facing the Qibla: One must face the Ka'bah while praying. Other conditions include purity of place, body, and clothes; covering the satr; the prayer time having entered; and making the niyyah (intention).

(v) Write any two benefits of Salah. ▼

1. Spiritual Benefit: Salah establishes a strong, permanent connection with Allah ﷻ, grants peace of heart through dhikr, forgives sins, and protects a person from evils (Surah Al-Ankabut 29:45).

2. Physical Benefit: Performing wudu five times a day keeps the body clean and fresh. The postures of ruku and sujud have medical benefits — in sujud, blood flows easily to the brain, refreshing it.

Q3. Detailed Question

Write a comprehensive note on the importance of Salah and its benefits & effects. ▼

Introduction: Salah is the second pillar of Islam, holding a unique place among the commands of Allah ﷻ. It was obligatory on every previous nation and is repeatedly commanded in the Quran (Surah Ar-Rum 30:31).

Status in Islam: The Prophet ﷺ called Salah the pillar (Amood) of the Deen (Tirmidhi 2616). The first question on the Day of Qiyamah will be about Salah (Ibn Majah 1425). Denying, doubting, or mocking it takes one out of Islam.

Philosophy: The fundamental purpose of Salah is to establish a strong spiritual connection with Allah ﷻ. In Salah, the servant is in private whisper with his Lord (Bukhari 417). It develops love, fear, and awe of Allah, humility through sujud, and the realization that man depends entirely on Allah's grace.

Khushu, Khuzu & Ikhlas: The soul of Salah is khushu (outer humility), khuzu (inner focus), and ikhlas (sincerity). Praying to show off is shirk (Musnad Ahmad 17140).

Jama'ah: The Quran commands "bow with those who bow" (2:43). Jama'ah prayer carries 27 times the reward of individual prayer (Bukhari 645). It builds discipline, obedience, brotherhood, and equality.

Benefits:

  • Spiritual: connection with Allah, peace through dhikr, forgiveness of sins, protection from evil (Surah Al-Ankabut 29:45).
  • Physical: cleanliness through wudu, health benefits of ruku and sujud (blood flow to the brain).
  • Social: obedience to the Ameer, love and sympathy among Muslims, equality in rows, revival of Islamic society.
A Muslim must establish Salah with khushu, khuzu, and ikhlas — in congregation whenever possible — to please Allah ﷻ and His Messenger ﷺ, and succeed in this world and the Hereafter.
