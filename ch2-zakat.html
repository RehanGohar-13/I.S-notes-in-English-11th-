Zakat & Sadaqat — The Philosophy of Charity

Chapter 2(B) — Ibadaat (Acts of Worship) · Philosophy of Zakat & Charitable Giving · Textbook pp. 43–46

بِسْمِ اللهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيْمِ "In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful."

📖 The Meaning of Zakat

The literal meaning of Zakat is "to be pure, to grow, and to increase."

In Islamic terminology, Zakat is a financial act of worship that is obligatory once a year, at the rate of 2.5%, on every sane, mature, free Muslim who is Sahib-e-Nisab.

A Muslim who pays Zakat purifies his wealth according to Allah's command, and his heart is purified from the greed of wealth.

خُذْ مِنْ اَمْوَالِهِمْ صَدَقَةً تُطَهِّرُهُمْ وَتُزَكِّيهِمْ "Take from their wealth a charity by which you purify them and cause them to increase (in righteousness)." Surah At-Tawbah 9:103
· · ·

⚖️ The Obligation of Zakat

  • Zakat became obligatory on 1st Ramadan, 2 AH.
  • The complete Zakat system was implemented in 9 AH, after the Conquest of Makkah.
  • Zakat is a fardh (obligatory) command — its denier leaves the fold of Islam.
  • After the demise of the Prophet ﷺ, some Arab tribes refused to pay Zakat to the Muslim government. The Companions (RA) waged Jihad against them and enforced this Islamic command.

Conditions for Zakat Being Obligatory

For Zakat to be binding on a person, these conditions must all be met:

  • Being sane (Aaqil)
  • Being mature (Baligh)
  • Being a free Muslim
  • Being Sahib-e-Nisab
  • A full lunar year (Qamari saal) having passed
  • Wealth being surplus to debts and basic necessities (household items, food, drink, clothing)
Important Rule: If the Nisab is complete at the start and end of the year, Zakat is obligatory. A dip in wealth during the year makes no difference. Zakat will be due on all wealth present at year-end.

💰 Nisab of Zakat (The Threshold)

Type of Wealth Nisab Rate
Silver 52.5 tola 2.5% (1/40)
Gold 7.5 tola 2.5% (1/40)
Cash, trade goods, surplus items Value of either silver or gold nisab 2.5%
Camels 5 camels Prescribed rate
Cows & Buffaloes 30 Prescribed rate
Goats 40 Prescribed rate
Animal Zakat: This applies to animals kept for breeding and that graze in open pastures for most of the year. Animals bought for sale are counted as trade goods (mal-e-tijarah) and follow trade-goods rulings.

Mal-e-Tijarah (trade goods): Any merchandise purchased with the intention of resale carries Zakat.

A Sahib-e-Nisab Muslim should calculate all assets annually and pay Zakat with full responsibility.

🎯 Masarif-e-Zakat (Where Zakat is Spent)

Along with commanding Zakat, Islam has also guided us to the eight categories on whom Zakat may be spent:

1️⃣ Fuqara (The Poor)

Those with very little wealth, below Nisab.

2️⃣ Masakeen (The Needy)

Those with nothing at all, in extreme need.

3️⃣ Aamileen

Those employed to collect and distribute Zakat.

4️⃣ Taleef-e-Qulub

Winning hearts towards Islam.

5️⃣ Freeing Slaves

Freeing those in bondage.

6️⃣ Debtors (Gharimeen)

Paying off the debts of those unable to repay.

7️⃣ Fi Sabilillah

In the path of Allah — matters that promote the Deen.

8️⃣ Musafir (Traveller)

A traveller stranded without means, even if wealthy at home.

· · ·

🌾 Ushr (The Tenth)

The literal meaning of Ushr is "one-tenth" (10%). In Islamic terminology, it is the Zakat of agricultural produce.

وَاٰتُوْا حَقَّهٗ يَوْمَ حَصَادِهٖ "And give its due (right) on the day of its harvest." Surah Al-An'am 6:141

Rates of Ushr

🌧️

10% — Full Ushr

Land irrigated by rain or natural springs
(One-tenth of produce)

🚰

5% — Half Ushr

Land irrigated by well water or artificial means
(One-twentieth of produce)

Important Distinctions:
  • Paying government tax does NOT fulfill Ushr.
  • Zakat is on wealth; Ushr is on land produce.
  • Zakat requires a lunar year to pass; Ushr does not.
  • If a field produces two or more harvests in a year, Ushr is due on every harvest.
· · ·

⚔️ Khums (The Fifth)

Khums means one-fifth (1/5) of wealth. Specifically, it refers to one-fifth of Mal-e-Ghanimah — wealth acquired from the enemies of Islam during battle.

This one-fifth share is designated for:

"And know that whatever you obtain as spoils of war — indeed, one-fifth of it is for Allah, for the Messenger ﷺ, for his close relatives, and for the orphans, the needy, and the traveller." Surah Al-Anfal 8:41

The 6 Shares of Khums

  1. Allah ﷻ
  2. Rasul ﷺ
  3. Relatives of the Rasul ﷺ
  4. Orphans (Yatama)
  5. The Needy (Masakeen)
  6. Travellers (Ibn-us-Sabeel)
· · ·

🤝 Responsibility of Aamileen-e-Zakat

Those who undertake the task of collecting Zakat and distributing it to the deserving must perform this duty with the utmost honesty (diyanat-dari).

Betrayal in collective wealth is an extremely grave crime.

The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: "Any person whom Allah has given responsibility over Muslims, and who dies while being treacherous and deceitful in that responsibility — Allah will forbid Jannah upon him." Sahih Bukhari: 7151

Recommendation: Rather than distributing Zakat individually, Muslims of means should arrange to collect Zakat at the community (muhalla) and government level and then distribute it among the proper categories of recipients.
· · ·

📊 Importance of Zakat in Islam's Economic System

Zakat holds the same status in the Islamic economic system as the soul holds in the human body. Its effects transform both individuals and society:

🌍 Societal Effects

  • End of poverty & unemployment
  • Formation of a welfare society (falahi mu'ashra)
  • Circulation of wealth — not confined to a few hands
  • Basic needs of all are met
  • Reduction in crime and oppression
  • End of begging (gada-gari)
  • Elimination of the class division

💗 Personal Effects

  • The remaining wealth becomes purified
  • Freedom from inner diseases like miserliness (bukhl)
  • Barakah in the wealth of the rich
  • Removal of sicknesses and calamities
  • Welfare of the poor and provision of life's essentials
Conclusion: If the system of Zakat, Sadaqat, Ushr, and Khums were properly implemented, poverty could be completely eradicated. Spending in Allah's path brings barakah to the wealth of the rich, removes diseases and calamities, and secures the welfare of the poor.

Q2. Short Questions

(i) Write any four Masarif-e-Zakat. ▼

Any four of the eight categories where Zakat may be spent:

  1. Fuqara — the poor
  2. Masakeen — the needy
  3. Aamileen — those employed to collect & distribute Zakat
  4. Taleef-e-Qulub — winning hearts towards Islam
  5. Fi Sabilillah — in the path of Allah (promoting the Deen)
  6. Musafir — stranded travellers
(ii) Write the Nisab of Zakat on gold and silver. ▼

Gold: 7.5 tola (approximately 87.48 grams).

Silver: 52.5 tola (approximately 612.36 grams).

On both, the rate of Zakat is 2.5% (one-fortieth) once the wealth has been held for a complete lunar year.

(iii) Describe the responsibilities of the Aamileen-e-Zakat. ▼

Aamileen-e-Zakat are those who undertake the duty of collecting Zakat and distributing it to the deserving. They must perform this duty with the utmost honesty (diyanat-dari), because betrayal in collective wealth is an extremely grave crime.

The Prophet ﷺ warned: "Any person whom Allah has given responsibility over Muslims, and who dies while being treacherous in that responsibility — Allah will forbid Jannah upon him." (Sahih Bukhari: 7151)

Hence Zakat should be collected and distributed with full transparency, preferably at the community or government level.

(iv) How is Islam's system of Zakat a guarantor of ending the class system? ▼

The Zakat system ensures the circulation of wealth so that it does not stay confined to the hands of a few. By annually transferring 2.5% of surplus wealth from the rich to the eight categories of recipients — particularly the poor and the needy — the gap between classes narrows significantly.

Basic needs of the deprived are fulfilled, poverty and unemployment decrease, begging ends, and a welfare society (falahi mu'ashra) is formed. This eliminates the social and economic distinction between the elite and the masses, establishing the Islamic principle of brotherhood and equality.

(v) If the price of 10 tola of gold is 30 lakh, how much Zakat will be due on it? ▼

Calculation:

  • Total value = Rs. 30,00,000 (30 lakh)
  • Zakat rate = 2.5% (1/40)
  • Zakat due = 30,00,000 × 2.5 ÷ 100 = Rs. 75,000

✅ Zakat payable = Rs. 75,000 (seventy-five thousand rupees).

Note: Since 10 tola of gold is well above the 7.5 tola Nisab, Zakat is fully due.

Q3. Detailed Question

Write a comprehensive note on the rulings, issues, and social effects of Zakat. ▼

Introduction: Zakat literally means "purity, growth, and increase." Islamically, it is a financial act of worship obligatory at the rate of 2.5% per year on every sane, mature, free Muslim who is Sahib-e-Nisab. Allah ﷻ commands: "Take from their wealth a charity by which you purify them…" (Surah At-Tawbah 9:103).

Obligation: Zakat became obligatory on 1st Ramadan 2 AH and the complete system was implemented in 9 AH. It is a fardh command; its denier leaves the fold of Islam. The Companions (RA) waged Jihad against the tribes who refused it after the Prophet's ﷺ demise.

Nisab: 7.5 tola of gold, 52.5 tola of silver, or their cash equivalent — along with trade goods and surplus items — all at the rate of 2.5%. Animal nisab is 5 camels, 30 cows/buffaloes, 40 goats (grazing & breeding). The nisab must be present at the beginning and end of the lunar year.

8 Masarif: Fuqara, Masakeen, Aamileen, Taleef-e-Qulub, freeing slaves, debtors, Fi Sabilillah, and travellers.

Related Obligations:

  • Ushr — 10% on naturally-irrigated land, 5% on artificially-irrigated land (Surah Al-An'am 6:141). Payable on every harvest.
  • Khums — 1/5 of Mal-e-Ghanimah divided among 6 categories (Surah Al-Anfal 8:41).

Aamileen Responsibility: Collection and distribution must be done with utter honesty. Betrayal is punished with denial of Jannah (Sahih Bukhari 7151).

Social & Economic Effects: Zakat is to Islam's economy what the soul is to the body. It:

  • Ends poverty and unemployment
  • Forms a welfare society
  • Keeps wealth in circulation
  • Reduces crime and oppression
  • Stops begging and class divisions
  • Purifies the wealth of the rich and their hearts from miserliness
  • Brings barakah, removes calamities, and secures the welfare of the poor
If the full system of Zakat, Sadaqat, Ushr, and Khums is properly implemented, poverty can be completely eradicated and a just, balanced economic society can be established — exactly as Islam envisions.
