Part II — Short Answer Questions (20 Marks)

Q2: Attempt any FIVE (5) short questions (5 × 2 = 10 Marks)

Write your answers in your test notebook first, then click any question header to verify key points.

1. Write two rights of parents mentioned in the Holy Quran or Hadith. ▼
  • Show kindness, obedience, and utmost respect without saying "Uff" or showing annoyance (Surah Al-Isra 17:23).
  • Pray continuously for Allah's forgiveness and mercy upon them.
2. Define Tauheed and state its fundamental significance in Islam. ▼
  • Definition: Firm belief that Allah is One, Unique, without any partner in His essence, attributes, or rights of worship.
  • Significance: It is the foundational pillar of Islam; all religious deeds and worship depend on it.
3. State two key provisions of the Charter of Madinah (Misaq-e-Madinah). ▼
  • Freedom of religion and equality before the law for all communities (Muslims and Jews).
  • Collective responsibility to jointly defend Madinah against outside invaders.
4. Briefly explain the concept of "Observance of Law" (Itteba-e-Qanoon) in Islam. ▼
  • Submitting completely to Divine Shariah and obeying state laws that maintain justice and order.
  • Ensuring that no individual, regardless of rank, is above the law (Surah An-Nisa 4:59).
5. What was the contribution of Hazrat Data Ganj Bakhsh Ali Hujwiri (RA) to Islamic preaching? ▼
  • Authored Kashf-ul-Mahjub, the earliest authoritative Persian book on Sufism and spiritual ethics.
  • Preached Islam in Lahore through moral purity and social service, converting thousands.
6. Mention two causes of the rise of Islamophobia in modern Western society. ▼
  • Sensationalist media bias and negative stereotyping of Islamic teachings.
  • Ignorance of authentic Islamic principles and political exploitation of anti-immigrant sentiment.
7. What is meant by "Halal Earnings" (Kasb-e-Halal) in Islamic teachings? ▼
  • Earning a living through honest, lawful work without interest (Riba), fraud, or bribery.
  • Declared a mandatory obligation after primary acts of worship (Kanz-ul-Ummal).
8. Write two scientific achievements of Muhammad ibn Musa al-Khwarizmi. ▼
  • Founded Algebra as an independent mathematical discipline (author of Al-Jabr wal-Muqabala).
  • Introduced Hindu-Arabic numerals and the positional decimal system (including zero) to the West.

Q3: Attempt any FIVE (5) short questions (5 × 2 = 10 Marks)

Write your answers in your test notebook first, then click any question header to verify key points.

1. Mention two rights of neighbors in Islamic society. ▼
  • Protecting their privacy, dignity, and property from any harm or disturbance.
  • Providing financial help, food, and emotional support during hardship.
2. What is meant by "Akhlaq-e-Hasanah" (Good Morals)? ▼
  • Noble character traits such as truthfulness, patience, humility, generosity, and forgiveness.
  • The Holy Prophet ﷺ stated that perfecting moral character was the primary purpose of his mission.
3. Briefly explain the role of Zakat in reducing economic inequality. ▼
  • Transfers surplus wealth from the wealthy to poor and needy members of society.
  • Prevents wealth hoarding and provides institutional social security.
4. State two key teachings of Hazrat Mujaddid Alf Sani (RA). ▼
  • Reformed Sufi philosophy by subjecting spiritual experiences strictly to Shariah (Wahdat-ush-Shuhud).
  • Strongly opposed court syncretism (Din-i-Ilahi) and preserved pure Islamic creed in Mughal India.
5. What is the role of Muslim youth in the Islamic Renaissance? ▼
  • Acquiring advanced scientific knowledge and technological skills while adhering to Islamic morals.
  • Leading social innovation, community service, and ethical civic engagement.
6. Write two duties of a citizen in an Islamic State. ▼
  • Obeying state laws and constitution in matters that do not violate Divine command.
  • Paying public taxes (Zakat/Ushr) and participating in defending the homeland.
7. Describe the role of Pir Syed Mehr Ali Shah (RA) against false doctrines. ▼
  • Authored Saif-e-Chishtiyai to defend the dogma of Khatm-e-Nabuwwat (Finality of Prophethood).
  • Challenged Mirza Ghulam Ahmad Qadiyani to a public scholarly debate in Lahore.
8. What is the significance of March 15 in the context of Islamophobia? ▼
  • Commemorates the 51 Muslim martyrs of the 2019 Christchurch mosque attacks in New Zealand.
  • Adopted by the UN General Assembly in 2022 as the International Day to Combat Islamophobia.

Part III — Detailed Questions (20 Marks)

Q4: Translate and explain any TWO (2) Hadiths (2 × 2 = 4 Marks)

Provide English translation and brief commentary for your chosen Hadiths.

Hadith 1: الْمُسْلِمُ مَنْ سَلِمَ الْمُسْلِمُونَ مِنْ لِسَانِهِ وَيَدِهِ ▼
الْمُسْلِمُ مَنْ سَلِمَ الْمُسْلِمُونَ مِنْ لِسَانِهِ وَيَدِهِ
"A true Muslim is the one from whose tongue and hand other Muslims are safe." (Sahih Bukhari)

Explanation: Emphasizes peaceful social conduct. True faith mandates refraining from backbiting, verbal abuse, slander, and physical aggression against others.

Hadith 2: طَلَبُ الْعِلْمِ فَرِيضَةٌ عَلَى كُلِّ مُسْلِمٍ ▼
طَلَبُ الْعِلْمِ فَرِيضَةٌ عَلَى كُلِّ مُسْلِمٍ
"Seeking knowledge is a mandatory obligation upon every Muslim." (Sunan Ibn Majah)

Explanation: Obligates both religious understanding and beneficial worldly knowledge for all Muslim men and women without gender discrimination.

Hadith 3: لاَ يَدْخُلُ الْجَنَّةَ مَنْ لاَ يَأْمَنُ جَارُهُ بَوَائِقَهُ ▼
لاَ يَدْخُلُ الْجَنَّةَ مَنْ لاَ يَأْمَنُ جَارُهُ بَوَائِقَهُ
"He will not enter Paradise whose neighbor is not safe from his harm and trouble." (Sahih Muslim)

Explanation: Establishes the sanctity of neighbors' rights in Islam, making peaceful coexistence with neighbors a prerequisite for sound faith.

Hadith 4: الدُّنْيَا سِجْنُ الْمُؤْمِنِ وَجَنَّةُ الْكَافِرِ ▼
الدُّنْيَا سِجْنُ الْمُؤْمِنِ وَجَنَّةُ الْكَافِرِ
"The world is a prison for the believer and a paradise for the disbeliever." (Sahih Muslim)

Explanation: Believers restrain their worldly desires within Divine boundaries in this temporary life to achieve eternal bliss in the Hereafter.

Q5: Attempt any TWO (2) detailed essay questions (2 × 8 = 16 Marks)

Click any essay topic below to review recommended outlines and key headings.

1. Discuss the Concept of Tauheed and its profound impact on human life and society. ▼

Recommended Essay Headings & Outline:

  • Introduction: Definition of Tauheed (Rububiyyah, Uluhiyyah, Asma-was-Sifat).
  • Quranic Evidences: Surah Al-Ikhlas (112:1-4) & Ayatul Kursi (2:255).
  • Individual Impact: Self-respect, freedom from fear/superstition, moral accountability, and humility.
  • Social Impact: Universal human equality, brotherhood, justice, and eradication of tyranny.
2. Write a comprehensive essay on the Rights of Non-Muslims in an Islamic State with historical references. ▼

Recommended Essay Headings & Outline:

  • Core Principles: Freedom of belief (2:256) & justice with non-belligerents (60:8).
  • Fundamental Rights: Protection of life, property, honor, and places of worship.
  • Historical Precedents: Charter of Madinah, Treaty of Najran, and Hazrat Umar's Jerusalem assurance.
  • Judicial Equality: Equal legal status in Islamic courts regardless of religion.
3. Explain Islamic Renaissance (Tajdeed-e-Ihya-e-Islam) and highlight the contributions of Muslim scientists. ▼

Recommended Essay Headings & Outline:

  • Concept of Renaissance: Intellectual, moral, and scientific revival of the Ummah.
  • Pioneering Figures & Contributions:
    • Ibn Sina: Medicine (Al-Qanun fi al-Tibb).
    • Al-Khwarizmi: Mathematics, Algebra, and positional decimal zero.
    • Al-Zahrawi: Father of modern surgical instruments.
    • Al-Biruni: Earth radius calculation and Indology.
  • Modern Revival: Integrating scientific research with Islamic moral values.
4. Analyze Islamophobia: examine its causes, real-world manifestations, and strategic steps to counter it. ▼

Recommended Essay Headings & Outline:

  • Definition: Irrational fear, prejudice, or hatred against Islam and Muslims.
  • Causes & Manifestations: Media bias, political exploitation, hate crimes (Christchurch 2019).
  • Global Legal Steps: UN March 15 Resolution (2022) & March 2024 measures against religious hatred.
  • Counter-Strategies: Demonstrating authentic Islamic character (Akhlaq), Paigham-e-Pakistan consensus model, active media engagement, and interfaith dialogue.
