Malaika — Belief in Angels

Chapter 2(C) — Faith (Imaniyat) · Belief in Angels · Textbook pp. 26–28

بِسْمِ اللهِ الرَّحْمٰنِ الرَّحِيْمِ "In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful."

🎯 Learning Outcomes

After studying this lesson, students will be able to:

  1. Understand the textual and rational proofs for belief in angels.
  2. Be aware of the attributes, rank, and worldly/otherworldly responsibilities of angels.
  3. Become firm believers in the angels.
  4. Become those who revere and respect the angels.
  5. Analyze the effects of firm belief in angels on one's life.

📖 What is Belief in Angels (Iman bil-Malaika)?

Iman bil-Malaika (Belief in Angels) means to firmly believe that angels are a creation of Allah made from light (Noor), and that they carry out every command of Allah without fail.

Belief in angels is essential for being a Muslim. Without it, the requirements of faith are incomplete.

Allah says:

وَلٰكِنَّ الْبِرَّ مَنْ آمَنَ بِاللّٰهِ وَالْيَوْمِ الْآخِرِ وَالْمَلَائِكَةِ وَالْكِتَابِ وَالنَّبِيِّينَ "But righteousness is [in] one who believes in Allah, the Last Day, the Angels, the Books, and the Prophets." Surah Al-Baqarah 2:177

🌟 Nature and Attributes of Angels

Key Attributes:

  • ✨ Created from Light (Noor): Unlike humans (clay) and Jinn (fire), angels are made of pure light.
  • 🚫 Sinless and Infallible: They are completely free from errors and sins.
  • 🙇 Perpetually Obedient: They are always engaged in the obedience and worship of Allah.
  • 🍽️ No Physical Needs: They do not eat, drink, or sleep.

Allah describes their absolute obedience:

لَا يَعْصُونَ اللّٰهَ مَا أَمَرَهُمْ وَيَفْعَلُونَ مَا يُؤْمَرُونَ "They do not disobey Allah in what He commands them, but do precisely what they are commanded." Surah At-Tahrim 66:6

The Prophet ﷺ said:

"The angels were created from light (Noor), the Jinn from a flame of fire, and Adam (AS) from clay (soil)." Sahih Muslim: 2996

Creation Comparison:
  • 👤 Humans (Adam AS) → Created from clay/soil
  • 🔥 Jinn → Created from smokeless fire
  • ✨ Angels → Created from light (Noor)

🔗 Connection Between Angels, Wahi, and Risalat

Allah chose the Prophets (AS) to deliver His commands to humanity. To deliver His guidance to the Prophets, Allah appointed His angels — especially the archangel Hazrat Jibra'eel (AS).

Because of this deep connection between Wahi (revelation) and the angels, believing in the angels is just as essential as believing in the Prophets and the Books. You cannot believe in the Quran without believing in the angel who brought it.

📋 Responsibilities and Duties of Angels

Angels perform numerous duties in this world by Allah's command, including protecting righteous servants, punishing the wicked, and recording deeds. Their number is vast — only Allah knows the exact count.

Staggering Numbers: During the Mi'raj, the Prophet ﷺ saw Bait-ul-Ma'moor (the Heavenly House) where 70,000 angels pray every single day — and once an angel enters and prays there, it never gets another turn. This tells us the number of angels is beyond human comprehension. Sahih Bukhari: 3207

The Four Archangels

🪽

1. Jibra'eel (AS)

The Chief Angel of Revelation. Delivers Allah's messages to the Prophets.

🌧️

2. Mika'eel (AS)

Responsible for rain, sustenance, and natural provisions.

📯

3. Israfeel (AS)

Will blow the Trumpet (Soor) on the Day of Judgment.

⚰️

4. Izra'eel (AS)

The Angel of Death — extracts the souls of the dying.

Other Angelic Groups and Their Duties

Angel Group Arabic Name Duty
Recording Angels کراماً کاتبین Record every good and bad deed of each human being.
Grave Questioners منکر نکیر Question the deceased in the grave about their faith (Munkar & Nakeer).
Guardian of Paradise رضوان The chief gatekeeper and guardian of Jannah (Rizwan).
Guardian of Hell مالک The chief guardian and warden of Jahannam (Malik).
Bearers of the Throne حاملینِ عرش Carry and support the Throne (Arsh) of Allah.
Angels of Punishment زبانیة Appointed to administer punishment in Hell (Zabaniyah).
Guardian Angels حافظین Protect humans from harm and dangerous things (Hafizeen).
Wandering Angels سیاحین Roam the earth seeking gatherings of righteous people and joining them (Sayyaheen).
Durood Deliverers — Carry the Durood (blessings) of the Ummah to the Prophet ﷺ.
Jumu'ah Recorders — Record the names of those who attend Friday prayers.

👤 Physical Appearances of Angels

Sometimes, by Allah's command, angels appear in human form:

  • When Hazrat Jibra'eel (AS) was sent to Hazrat Maryam (AS), he appeared as an ordinary human man.
  • Hazrat Jibra'eel (AS) frequently appeared before the Prophet ﷺ in the form of the Companion Hazrat Dihya Kalbi (RA), who was known for his handsome appearance.
· · ·

💡 Why Belief in Angels Matters

Allah does not need angels to carry out His commands — He can execute His will with a single word. He created angels out of His infinite wisdom and assigned them various duties.

Belief in angels produces two powerful effects:

  • 🏔️ Awe of Allah's Greatness: If the Creator made such powerful and magnificent beings, imagine how infinitely greater the Creator Himself must be!
  • 🤲 Gratitude to Allah: Allah has appointed angels for our benefit, protection, and service — recording our deeds, guarding us from harm, and delivering our prayers to the Prophet ﷺ. A believer is deeply grateful for this divine arrangement.

Q2. Short Questions

(i) What is meant by belief in angels? ▼

Belief in angels (Iman bil-Malaika) means to firmly believe that angels are a creation of Allah made from light (Noor), that they are sinless, perpetually obedient, and carry out every command of Allah without fail. This belief is an essential pillar of Iman — without it, faith is incomplete (2:177).

(ii) Write any two attributes of angels. ▼

1. Angels are created from light (Noor) and have no physical needs like eating, drinking, or sleeping.

2. Angels are completely sinless and infallible. They never disobey Allah and are always engaged in His worship and obedience (66:6).

(iii) Write any two responsibilities of angels. ▼

1. Kiraman Katibeen: Recording every good and bad deed of each human being.

2. Hafizeen: Protecting humans from harm and dangerous things by Allah's command.

(iv) Write the translation of Quranic verses related to belief in angels. ▼

Surah Al-Baqarah 2:177: "But righteousness is [in] one who believes in Allah, the Last Day, the Angels, the Books, and the Prophets."

Surah At-Tahrim 66:6: "They do not disobey Allah in what He commands them, but do precisely what they are commanded."

(v) What effects does belief in angels have on human life? ▼

Belief in angels produces two major effects: (1) It creates a deep sense of awe and awareness of Allah's greatness — if the Creator made such powerful beings, how infinitely greater must He be! (2) It generates gratitude to Allah — knowing that He has appointed angels for our protection, record-keeping, and service makes a believer deeply thankful.

Q3. Detailed Question

Write a detailed note on belief in angels and their responsibilities. ▼

Definition: Iman bil-Malaika means believing that angels are Allah's creation made from light (Noor), sinless, and perpetually obedient. This belief is a fundamental pillar of Iman (2:177).

Nature: Angels do not eat, drink, or sleep. They never disobey Allah (66:6). The Prophet ﷺ confirmed their creation from light (Muslim: 2996).

Numbers: Their number is vast beyond comprehension. At Bait-ul-Ma'moor, 70,000 angels pray daily, each only once (Bukhari: 3207).

Four Archangels: Jibra'eel (revelation), Mika'eel (sustenance/rain), Israfeel (trumpet), Izra'eel (death).

Key Angelic Groups:

  • Kiraman Katibeen — Record human deeds
  • Munkar & Nakeer — Question the dead in the grave
  • Rizwan — Guardian of Paradise
  • Malik — Guardian of Hell
  • Hamilin-e-Arsh — Bearers of Allah's Throne
  • Zabaniyah — Angels of punishment
  • Hafizeen — Guardian angels protecting humans
  • Sayyaheen — Wandering angels seeking righteous gatherings

Impact: Belief in angels instills awe of Allah's greatness and deep gratitude for His divine arrangement of protection and service.
